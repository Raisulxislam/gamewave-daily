Just when you thought the post-Gamescom livestream lull had settled in, Sony went and dropped a surprise: **PlayStation State of Play** is coming back with two back-to-back broadcasts in September 2026. No rest for the hype cycle.

The dual-broadcast format is unusual for State of Play, which has traditionally favored single, focused events. Running two shows back to back suggests Sony has a lot on the docket — potentially splitting spotlight between first-party PlayStation Studios titles and third-party partner reveals. If you've been waiting for concrete updates on projects like *Marvel's Wolverine* or other long-teased exclusives, this might finally be the showcase that delivers.

The first broadcast is expected to lead with PS5 and PS5 Pro titles already in the pipeline, while the second could spotlight smaller indie gems and live-service updates. Sony has learned to manage expectations carefully after a few State of Play events that felt underwhelming, so expect a tighter, more curated presentation this time around.

Exact start times and viewing links should be confirmed in the coming days. For now, mark your calendar — Sony is clearly not letting anyone enjoy a quiet September.
