## More Than a Budget Play

Lenovo invited us to New York City earlier this summer to see the IdeaPad Vibe lineup in person, and the impression it left was unexpectedly strong. Starting at just under $700, these machines don't feel like budget compromises — they feel like a company that's figured out how to deliver premium aesthetics at a mass-market price.

The hardware genuinely surprised. Build quality, keyboard travel, and display sharpness all punch above what the price tag suggests. **But the real differentiator is personalization.** Lenovo is offering an unprecedented level of color and configuration variety, making the decision of which Vibe to buy arguably harder than choosing the CPU inside it.

## Taking On the MacBook Neo

At this price point, the IdeaPad Vibe squares off directly against Apple's MacBook Neo and Dell's newest 14-inch competitor. Where those machines lean on conservative design language, Lenovo is betting that buyers — especially younger ones — want their laptop to actually look like something they chose, not something they settled for.
