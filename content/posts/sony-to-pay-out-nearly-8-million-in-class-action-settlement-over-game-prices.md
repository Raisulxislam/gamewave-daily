Sony has agreed to a class-action settlement worth nearly $8 million after a lawsuit challenged how the company priced digital PlayStation games. The deal, first reported by IGN, follows a federal judge's rejection of Sony's motion to dismiss the case earlier this year.

## Who Qualifies and What It Means

Customers who purchased digital PlayStation games between 2019 and 2023 are potentially eligible for a payout under the settlement terms. The class covers millions of transactions across the PlayStation Store during that four-year window, making this one of the largest consumer pricing settlements in the gaming industry.

## The Bigger Picture for Digital Game Pricing

The lawsuit originally argued that Sony used its market dominance to inflate digital storefront prices, a complaint echoed across the industry as console makers have shifted more revenue toward digital sales. While the settlement does not admit wrongdoing, it signals that platform holders face real legal exposure when their pricing practices draw scrutiny.
