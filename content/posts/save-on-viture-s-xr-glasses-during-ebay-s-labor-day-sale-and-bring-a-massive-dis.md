## A Giant Screen That Fits in Your Pocket — At Last, at a Real Price

VITURE's XR glasses project a massive virtual display — up to 135 inches — directly in front of your eyes, turning any USB-C device into a portable theater or gaming rig. The catch has always been cost: new models run $200 to $550. But this Labor Day, eBay's VITURE authorized storefront is slashing prices on refurbished units to their lowest levels yet.

The standout deal is the VITURE Pro XR/AR, which drops to around $143 with a coupon code — roughly 30% off the standard refurbished price and nearly half off retail. The Luma Pro, Luma Ultra, and Beast models are also discounted, with some reaching 48% off when stacked with eBay's coupon offers. Every refurbished pair ships with a two-year Allstate warranty and free shipping.

## What You're Actually Getting

The Pro model packs a 120Hz Micro-OLED display at full 1080p per eye, electrochromic light blocking, and HARMAN-tuned stereo speakers. It works with Android, iPhone 16/15, MacBook, and any USB-C device. At $143, you're getting premium AR eyewear at a price that undercuts most mid-range gaming monitors — except this one goes wherever you do.

If you've been curious about XR glasses but balking at the price, this is the moment. The refurbished stock won't last long at these rates.
