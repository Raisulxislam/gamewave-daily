Capcom has never been shy about fan service, but this is a new level: for Resident Evil's 30th anniversary exhibition, the studio is building life-sized statues of Lady Dimitrescu and Albert Wesker. The internet, predictably, is being entirely normal about it. For a series that debuted in 1996, one display room is about to hold two very different generations of Resident Evil at full scale.

## Two Generations of Resident Evil, in One Room

Dimitrescu became a pop-culture event almost overnight when Resident Evil Village arrived in 2021, using about nine feet of pure theatrical menace. Wesker, meanwhile, has been gaming's favourite sunglasses-wearing supervillain for most of the past two decades. Together they bracket the thing this franchise does better than almost anyone: horror with one foot in camp.

That is evidently exactly how the audience is receiving the news. The replies are thirsty, chaotic and entirely on brand — a completely normal Resident Evil fandom Tuesday, which is to say ideal marketing for a 30th birthday.
