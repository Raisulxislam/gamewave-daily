Crunchyroll's Game Vault is quietly becoming one of the bigger deals in mobile gaming, and one of the coziest games of the year is riding along for the ride.

## The Vault Keeps Adding More

The anime streaming brand has announced another wave of additions to its Game Vault, the benefits service bundled with its membership. For a few extra dollars a month, subscribers get a rotating library of mobile games — and the list keeps getting longer.

## A Cozy Crowd-Puller

Among the names joining the lineup is one of 2026's warmest, most relaxing indie releases. For a service built on anime fandom, these gentle, low-stress games are a smart fit: they cost little to make, travel well on phones, and give the vault a reason to feel fresh.

It is a slow-burn growth story, but a real one. Crunchyroll has turned a simple perk into a reason to stay — and cozy gaming is helping it get there.
