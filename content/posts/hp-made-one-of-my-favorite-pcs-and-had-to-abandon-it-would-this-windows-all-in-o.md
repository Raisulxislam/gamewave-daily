## The All-in-One That Actually Moved

HP's Envy Move was one of those rare PC ideas that felt genuinely clever the moment you saw it: a full Windows all-in-one desktop with a built-in battery, designed to be carried from room to room like a much more elegant television. It wasn't trying to replace your tower — it was solving a problem you didn't know you had, and doing it with surprising grace.

## A Concept That Deserves a Second Act

HP ultimately discontinued the Envy Move, and that feels like a genuine loss. The PC all-in-one category has long been stuck in a design rut — sleek slabs that never leave one desk — and the Envy Move's portability twist was the first fresh idea the space had seen in years. If HP ever revisited the concept with modern internals, there's a real audience waiting for it. Sometimes the best ideas just arrive a little too early.
