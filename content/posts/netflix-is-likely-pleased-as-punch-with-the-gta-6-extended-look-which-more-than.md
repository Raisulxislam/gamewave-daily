Netflix has every reason to be grinning. The extended GTA 6 showcase that aired on the platform more than doubled the streamer's web traffic — a spike the company is understandably thrilled about.

**A win for the streamer**

When Rockstar chose Netflix to debut the longer look at the next Grand Theft Auto, it handed the platform a genuine cultural moment. The numbers back that up: visits to Netflix's digital real estate roughly doubled, proving just how much pull a franchise this size still has.

**Why it adds up**

For Netflix, this is exposure money can't easily buy — a single broadcast that drags in new eyes and makes the service feel like the default home for big gaming moments. Expect the streamer to chase more of exactly this kind of deal.
