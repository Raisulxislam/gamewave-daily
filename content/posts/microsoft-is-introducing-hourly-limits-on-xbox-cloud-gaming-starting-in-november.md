## What's changing in November

Starting in November 2026, Xbox Game Pass Ultimate subscribers will be limited to 15 hours of cloud gaming per month. The change applies exclusively to streaming via Xbox Cloud Gaming — local console and PC play remain completely unaffected. Additional streaming hours will be available for purchase, though Microsoft hasn't yet confirmed pricing.

The 15-hour limit represents a major shift from Microsoft's original pitch for cloud gaming, which positioned unlimited streaming as a core selling point of the Ultimate tier. But the company says the vast majority of subscribers use fewer than 15 hours of cloud play per month, making this a boundary that most users will never actually hit.

## How to make the hours count

**Think of it less as a restriction and more as a nudge toward intentional play.** If you're the kind of subscriber who streams a quick game during a lunch break or commutes with cloud play on a phone, 15 hours is roughly half an hour a day — far more than the average use case.

For power users who routinely blow past that ceiling, the add-on purchase model creates a pay-as-you-go safety net. Microsoft is clearly testing whether cloud gaming can transition from a loss-leading perk into a revenue stream of its own. The November launch will be the first real data point on whether that bet pays off.
