## Epic Parties breaks down the walls between platforms

Epic Games has officially launched Epic Parties, a cross-platform voice chat system designed to keep friends connected regardless of what they're playing or where. The feature allows players on PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, PC, and mobile to maintain a persistent voice conversation even when they're in entirely different games — a level of social flexibility that no single platform has offered natively before.

## Why this matters for online gaming

Epic's move is quietly significant. While platform holders like Sony and Microsoft have individually improved their party chat systems, none have built a truly cross-ecosystem layer that sits above the walled gardens. Epic Parties doesn't replace in-game voice — it supplements it, letting squads stay in touch during solo sessions, match queues, or when one friend logs into something completely different. For the growing number of households where friends split across consoles, this is the kind of infrastructure that makes the social side of gaming actually work.
