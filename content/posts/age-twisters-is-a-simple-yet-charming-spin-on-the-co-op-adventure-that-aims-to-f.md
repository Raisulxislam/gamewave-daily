## A Co-op Adventure That Aims for Pixar Warmth

Age Twisters is not here to bruise your brain. The new co-op adventure from Piccolo Studios is a deliberately light, winsome take on the genre — a game built less around head-scratching challenge and more around the shared delight of moving through a story together.

That's a deliberate design choice, and the studio says as much. "The puzzles won't stump you," Piccolo Studios acknowledges about this co-op adventure — "but they're not supposed to." The point isn't to test your wits; it's to keep the story flowing at a warm, unhurried pace, the way an animated feature manages its beats.

## Easy Puzzles, Big Heart

This is a quiet but refreshing counter to co-op gaming's obsession with escalating difficulty. Not every game needs a poe-razor difficulty curve; sometimes the best table for two is one where neither player feels left behind. Age Twisters aims squarely at that audience, positioning itself as a game you can finish on a rainy evening with a good friend beside you, trading the thrill of hard-won victory for the glow of shared experience.

The "feels like watching a Pixar movie" aim is the tell here. That's an ambitious emotional standard for a puzzle adventure to reach, and reaching for it — even if imperfectly — is a more interesting goal than another hundred hours of gray-box green puzzles. Whether its charms carry a full playthrough remains to be seen, but the intent is unmistakably sweet.

For anyone tired of co-op games that treat failure as the price of admission, Age Twisters makes a gentle, persuasive case for the opposite.
