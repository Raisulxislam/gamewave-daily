In a revelation that connects two of gaming's most beloved franchises, Respawn Entertainment's upcoming Star Wars Zero Company was originally conceived as a game set in the Titanfall universe. The project underwent a significant creative pivot before arriving at its current form as a squad-based Star Wars tactical title.

## From Mechs to Lightsabers

The Titanfall franchise has been dormant since 2016's Titanfall 2, despite persistent fan demand for a third installment. Repurposing the initial pitch as a Star Wars game allowed Respawn to leverage its combat design expertise within one of entertainment's biggest properties. The studio declined to share details about how much of the original Titanfall DNA survives in Zero Company's final design.

## What This Tells Us About Respawn's Strategy

The pivot underscores how major publishers often recycle creative concepts across franchises rather than abandon them entirely. For Star Wars fans, it raises an intriguing question: does Zero Company's tactical gameplay carry echoes of Titanfall's frenetic pacing beneath its Jedi robes?
