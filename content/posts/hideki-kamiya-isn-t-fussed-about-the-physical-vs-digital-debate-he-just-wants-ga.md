## The Creator of Bayonetta Weighs In

Hideki Kamiya — the director behind Resident Evil 2 and the creator of Bayonetta — has weighed in on one of gaming's most persistent debates, and his stance is refreshingly pragmatic. In a recent interview, Kamiya said he is not particularly fussed about whether games exist on physical discs or as digital downloads. What matters to him, he explained, is that games are preserved so future generations can experience them.

It is a perspective that cuts through the tribalism of the physical-vs-digital argument. **Kamiya is not picking a side — he is reframing the question entirely.** The real issue is not format; it is access. If a game disappears because a storefront shuts down or a disc becomes unreadable, the medium has failed the art.

## Preservation Over Format Wars

Coming from a developer whose career spans the transition from cartridges to optical media to digital distribution, the statement carries weight. Kamiya has seen formats come and go, and his concern is not nostalgic — it is practical. **Games should outlive the hardware they run on**, and whether that happens through disc-to-digital conversions, official re-releases, or something else entirely, the goal remains the same: let people play the games that shaped the medium.
