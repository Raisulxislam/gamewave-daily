## A Role Fans Never Got to See

Shailene Woodley is revisiting one of Hollywood's more notable what-ifs: her turn as Mary Jane Watson in *The Amazing Spider-Man 2*. The actress filmed a handful of scenes as Peter Parker's iconic love interest for the 2014 sequel, only for director Marc Webb to ultimately cut the character from the finished film in the interest of tightening a crowded narrative.

In a recent conversation, Woodley described the experience as strange but formative. She had already completed the work and bonded with co-star Andrew Garfield before the decision came down — the kind of professional disappointment that tests an actor's resilience early in a career that, for Woodley, was about to accelerate rapidly with *Divergent* and *The Fault in Our Stars*.

## The Bigger Spider-Man Shuffle

The decision to cut Mary Jane was less about Woodley's performance and more about a franchise trying to launch too many threads at once. *The Amazing Spider-Man 2* juggled the return of Electro, the debut of Harry Osborn as Green Goblin, and the death of Gwen Stacy — and the filmmakers ultimately decided that adding another major character arc would dilute the emotional core. It remains one of the clearest examples of a studio choosing restraint, even if the broader film was anything but restrained in other ways.
