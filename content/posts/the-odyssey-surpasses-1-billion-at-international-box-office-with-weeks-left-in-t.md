Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* has officially crossed the $1 billion mark at the international box office — a milestone that cements it as one of the year's biggest theatrical events. The film still has nearly four weeks left in its scheduled theatrical window, which means it has a real shot at climbing significantly higher before it hits premium VOD.

It's not a franchise installment designed to sell the next four sequels. It's an original, director-driven blockbuster — and it's making a billion dollars.
