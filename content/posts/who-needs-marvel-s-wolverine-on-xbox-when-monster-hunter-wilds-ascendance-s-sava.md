## Capcom Keeps the Hype Train Rolling

Capcom has dropped the next entry in its **Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance** weapon showcase series, and this time the Dual Blades are in the spotlight. The trailer highlights how the blade's signature speed and aggression will be amplified by the all-new Boost Bracer mechanic — a system that promises to add a fresh layer of risk-reward to an already adrenaline-fueled moveset.

The Dual Blades have always been the series' most visceral weapon: short-range, stamina-hungry, and unapologetically fast. Pairing that identity with a mechanic that rewards aggressive timing feels like a natural evolution rather than a gimmick. If the Boost Bracer delivers on what the trailer suggests, Dual Blade mains are about to have their best iteration yet.

## Building Toward Launch

Capcom has been rolling these weapon showcases out methodically, and each one has landed with considerable community buzz. The Ascendance expansion is shaping up to be a substantial evolution of the Wilds formula, and these deep dives are doing their job — making the wait until launch feel almost unbearable. **Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance** continues to look like one of the biggest action RPG events of 2026.
