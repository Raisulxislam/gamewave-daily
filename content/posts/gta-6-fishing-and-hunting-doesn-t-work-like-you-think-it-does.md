Rockstar Games has confirmed new details about GTA 6 fishing and hunting mechanics, revealing that the activities won't work exactly as fans might expect. Speaking to YouTuber TGG, Rockstar North's Rob Nelson explained that fishing in GTA 6 is strictly catch-and-release—players cannot keep any fish they catch.

## Rockstar Confirms Catch-and-Release Fishing

Nelson emphasized that the decision aligns with the game's realistic portrayal of Miami's ecosystem, where overfishing could harm the environment. This mechanic adds a layer of moral consideration, encouraging players to engage with nature responsibly.

## Hunting Works Differently Than Expected

While fishing is limited to catch-and-release, hunting in GTA 6 introduces a different set of rules. According to Nelson, hunting will focus on tracking and observing wildlife rather than simply shooting animals. This approach aims to create a more immersive and respectful experience, reflecting Rockstar's commitment to authentic gameplay.

These revelations come as part of a broader push to make GTA 6's open world feel more alive and interactive. With fishing and hunting serving as optional activities, players can choose to explore the natural world at their own pace, adding depth to the game's sprawling environment.
