## Samsung Quietly Killed Its Best Mid-Range OLED Feature

If you've been eyeing the Samsung S90F, here's a detail that matters: the 2026 successor, the S90H, drops quantum-dot OLED technology from the S90 lineup entirely. The 55-, 65-, and 77-inch S90F are the last models in this tier to feature QD-OLED panels, which deliver meaningfully better color volume and HDR brightness than the WOLED displays Samsung is switching to. In testing, the S90F covered roughly 89% of the BT.2020 color gamut versus the LG C5's 76% — and that gap was entirely thanks to quantum dots.

Translation for gamers: the S90F supports 4K at 120fps with HDMI 2.1, VRR, and ALLM on all four ports. That's everything you need for PS5 or Xbox Series X at full tilt.

## Labor Day Deal: $1,038 on eBay

For a limited time, Electronic Express — an authorized Samsung reseller — is selling the 65" S90F for $1,038.39 through eBay using coupon code "LABORDAY20" for 20% off. That's a 1-year manufacturer warranty included. If you want quantum-dot color in a mid-range OLED this year, you'll otherwise need to step up to the S95H at $2,800.

The S90F's glossy panel also has a quiet advantage over the S90H's new matte "Glare-Free" coating: deeper perceived blacks and stronger contrast in dark rooms. For home theater setups, the older model is arguably the more cinematic pick. The trade-off is that the S90H gets 165Hz gaming and Wi-Fi 6E — but unless you're gaming on a high-end PC, the S90F's 120Hz QD-OLED panel is the better value proposition at this price.
