The archer may not be done yet. Jeremy Renner, who has played Clint Barton since 2011, appears to be hinting at a comeback just as a final wave of Avengers: Doomsday reshoots is reportedly getting underway.

**A timely tease**

In a post that fans were quick to decode, Renner references a return to a familiar hooded look — the sort of breadcrumb the MCU faithful pounce on. Set against chatter about Doomsday's extra filming, the timing feels deliberate, even if Marvel itself stays silent for now.

**Why it matters**

The original Hawkeye has been largely off-screen since the events of the Disney+ series, leaving his fate ambiguous within the wider saga. A landing spot in Doomsday's reshoots would slot him neatly into the multiverse reckoning that's shaping the next phase of the Avengers story.
