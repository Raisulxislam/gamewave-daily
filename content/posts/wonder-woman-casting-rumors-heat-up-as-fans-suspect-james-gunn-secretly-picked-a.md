## Fan Detectives Are Working Overtime

Wonder Woman casting rumors have reached a fever pitch after DC fans noticed what they believe are deliberate breadcrumbs from Adria Arjona. The actress recently shared a video of her workout routine — posted, intriguingly, just ahead of the DCU's upcoming *Man of Tomorrow* — and the internet collectively decided this was anything but a coincidence.

The theory running through DC fan communities is straightforward: James Gunn has already locked in his Wonder Woman, and Arjona's social media activity is a soft reveal in disguise. It's the kind of fan sleuthing that could be entirely wrong, but the timing and circumstantial evidence have made it one of the most discussed casting whispers of the year.

## What We Actually Know

As of now, neither Gunn nor Warner Bros. have officially confirmed any casting for the DCU's Wonder Woman. What is confirmed is that *Man of Tomorrow* — the Superman sequel — is in active production and will be a key piece in establishing the broader DCU slate. Whether Arjona ends up wielding the lasso or not, the intensity of fan engagement around **Wonder Woman casting** shows just how much pressure sits on Gunn's next reveal.
