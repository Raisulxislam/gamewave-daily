I swear it came like that — no, really. Pulsar's new mouse is literally sliced in half down the middle, and the designer's logic is refreshingly unhinged: cut the shell, lose the grams.

**Half a shell, twice the bravado**

By removing a central spine of material, Pulsar has chased a weight that borders on the genuinely crazy-light, positioning the release to topple the existing record for the lightest gaming mouse on the market.

**Fair warning**

It's a bold engineering statement, but it won't suit everyone — expect serious fans of featherweight hardware to line up fast, while those who like a bit of palm-filling heft may want to look away.
