Star Wars Zero Company is almost upon us, and if you're anything like us, you've already spent hours theorycrafting loadouts before the game even launches. The starting class you pick in Zero Company isn't just cosmetic — it shapes your entire early-game rhythm, from which Force abilities you unlock first to how resilient you are against the first wave of Imperial opposition.

## The heavy hitters vs. the subtle operators

Zero Company's class roster splits neatly into two camps: bruisers who want to wade into a firefight and duellists who prefer to pick their moment. The Commando archetype, for instance, leans hard into sustained firepower and grenade uptime, making it a reliable pick for players who want to clear rooms methodically. On the other end, the Infiltrator trades raw durability for stealth bonuses and faster ability cooldowns, rewarding players who like to thin the herd before the real fight begins.

## So which one should you actually pick?

Honestly? It depends on whether you'd rather dominate a skirmish head-on or outmanoeuvre the Empire entirely. Both paths open up viable builds by mid-game, but the early hours will feel radically different depending on your choice.
