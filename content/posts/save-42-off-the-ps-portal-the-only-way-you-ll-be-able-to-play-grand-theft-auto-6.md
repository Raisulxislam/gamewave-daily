Here's a deal worth paying attention to: the PlayStation Portal is down 42% to around $150. And if you're planning to play Grand Theft Auto 6 on something smaller than your TV, this may genuinely be the only way to do it on a handheld.

GTA 6 has been confirmed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S at launch — no PC, no Switch 2, no handheld of any kind. That means the PS Portal, which streams games from your PS5 over Wi-Fi, is currently the only device that lets you take Rockstar's next blockbuster on the go, assuming your console is powered on at home.

## The Portal's Timing Couldn't Be Better

At $150, the Portal sits in impulse-buy territory for a lot of gamers, and its value proposition just got significantly sharper with GTA 6 on the horizon. The device has always been a tough sell at full price — it's a streaming handheld with no native processing — but at 42% off, the calculus changes.

Of course, you'll still need a PS5, a solid Wi-Fi connection, and the patience to deal with streaming latency. But for players who want GTA 6 on a screen they can hold, this is the deal that makes it possible.
