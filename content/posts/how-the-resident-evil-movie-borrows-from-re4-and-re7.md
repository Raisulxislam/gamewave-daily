Resident Evil is heading to theaters later this month, and director Zach Cregger is pulling back the curtain on the creative DNA behind the film. In a recent Reddit Ask Me Anything session, Cregger revealed that both Resident Evil 4 and Resident Evil 7 played significant roles in shaping the movie's tone and direction.

The choice of source material is telling. RE4 marked the franchise's pivot from pure survival horror into action-driven intensity, while RE7 stripped everything back to claustrophobic dread and first-person vulnerability. Borrowing from both suggests Cregger is aiming for a film that shifts between dread and momentum — not locked into one mode.

## A Game-Literate Director

Cregger's willingness to cite specific game influences sets this apart from previous Resident Evil adaptations, which often wore the brand name while ignoring what made the games work. A director who actually understands why RE4's over-the-shoulder tension and RE7's isolated terror resonated has a far better shot at translating that feeling to screen.

Whether that translates to a good movie remains to be seen — but it's the most encouraging creative signal this franchise has received in a long time.
