There was plenty of buzz circling Silver Palace at gamescom before the show floor even opened — more than 10 million pre-registrations worldwide, per Elementa. At Opening Night Live the developer laid out its biggest swing yet: a moody new trailer, a shadowy multiplayer tease called House of Greed, and one genuinely surprising announcement. Silver Palace is coming to PlayStation 5 as well as the previously confirmed PC and mobile versions.

## Silver Palace at gamescom: Hands-On With Silvernia

The playable demo did the heavy lifting. Flying silver whales drift over the ornate spires of Silvernia, a failed assassination attempt on the Queen erupts into a boss battle between portal-born giants, and a second showdown against a twisted Cinderella — swinging her pumpkin carriage like a weapon — shows off the four-character squad and real-time switching. The horror-tinged, Resident Evil-flavored trailer tone isn't a pivot, the team insists: it's a deliberate contrast meant to stress the gap between fairytale heroes and the monsters stalking their streets.

## House of Greed and What Comes Next

The trailer's loudest tease was House of Greed, an early-stage multiplayer mode built around an over-the-shoulder camera, room-based exploration and third-person shooting — a clear departure from the main campaign's squad brawling. On the floor, the demo ran on RTX 5080 machines with DLSS 4.5 and ray tracing switched on. Add a gamescom Best Mobile Game nomination and a third closed beta on the way, and this fairytale is clearly just warming up.
