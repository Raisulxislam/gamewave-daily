## All Paths Lead Somewhere — But Not Everywhere

The Blood of Dawnwalker doesn't do easy answers. The game's multiple endings aren't just cosmetic variations on the same finale — they reflect the full weight of your decisions across its narrative arc. Whether you've finished a first playthrough or you're mapping out your second, here's what you need to know about reaching every conclusion.

There are **three primary endings** in The Blood of Dawnwalker, each tied to a pivotal choice made in the final act. Without spoiling the specifics of each path, the determining factor comes down to how you handle the conflict between the Dawnwalker order and the forces you've encountered throughout the story. One path favors preservation, one embraces sacrifice, and one leans into power — but each has consequences that ripple through the world state.

## How to Get Each Ending

To trigger a specific ending, pay close attention to the faction alignment quests in chapters 7 through 9. Your cumulative relationship scores with key characters — particularly the ones you've helped or hindered along the way — lock in which path becomes available. The final choice itself is a single, irreversible decision at the end of the last chapter, so make sure you've saved before committing.

If you're hunting for the completionist ending, focus on maximizing your bond with at least two companion characters before the climactic sequence. Each ending also unlocks unique gear and a different post-credits scene, giving you a solid reason to revisit the story at least twice.
