## Your PS5 Is Already a Free Streaming Hub

Most PS5 owners treat their console as a gaming machine and nothing else. That's a missed opportunity. Sony has quietly bundled access to several free streaming services directly into the PlayStation 5 ecosystem, and if you haven't poked around the Media tab lately, you're leaving entertainment on the table.

## What You Actually Get for Free

Services like Pluto TV, Tubi, and Crackle offer ad-supported libraries of movies and TV shows at zero cost — no subscription required. The PS5's built-in media apps make launching these services seamless, and the DualSense controller doubles as a surprisingly competent remote for browsing. Combined with Sony's own PlayStation Plus tiers, which sometimes include streaming perks, your console can cover a solid chunk of your viewing habits without额外 spending a dime.

The catch, as always, is ads. Free tier streaming means sitting through commercial breaks, which is the trade-off for not paying monthly. Still, for casual viewing or background noise while gaming installs, it's a legitimately useful setup that most people overlook.
