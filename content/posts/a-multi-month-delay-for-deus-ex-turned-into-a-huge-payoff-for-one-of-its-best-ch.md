## The Delay That Paid Off

Most developers treat delays as a necessary evil. Harvey Smith, the veteran designer behind Deus Ex, sees at least one as a creative blessing. Speaking about the game's late-stage development, Smith shared how **a multi-month delay** gave the team the breathing room it needed to elevate one of the franchise's most memorable characters from good to genuinely great.

Smith acknowledged that the story takes some winding to reach its punchline — but it's worth the ride. The takeaway is straightforward: extra development time isn't automatically wasted when the team knows where to aim it.

Deus Ex remains one of the most cited examples in game-development postmortems, and stories like this reinforce why. Even the scheduling setbacks turned into design wins.
