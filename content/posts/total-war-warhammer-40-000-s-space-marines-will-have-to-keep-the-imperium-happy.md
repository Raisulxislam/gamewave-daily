## Even Transhuman Warriors Have to Play Politics

Creative Assembly is adding a layer of Imperial reputation management to Total War: Warhammer 40,000, and it fundamentally changes how you'll think about commanding a Space Marine chapter. Your forces won't just be judged on battlefield performance — they'll need to maintain the Imperium's approval and avoid behaviors that could be construed as heretical. For a franchise built on tactical military conquest, that's a genuinely bold design pivot.

## Heresy Has Consequences

This means players will face meaningful trade-offs between raw military efficiency and political survival. Cutting corners, allying with questionable factions, or straying from Imperial dogma could carry real penalties downstream. It's a mechanic that aligns beautifully with the Warhammer 40,000 lore, where the Inquisition's suspicion has ended far mightier crusades than any xenos threat. Creative Assembly appears to be building a Total War that rewards restraint as much as aggression — and that could be the most interesting thing about this entry.
