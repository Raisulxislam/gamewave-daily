There's a quiet poetry in turning instruments of war into pocket-sized tokens of peace. GSC Game World, the Ukrainian studio behind S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl, has launched a fundraiser built around a visceral piece of the conflict raging near its front door — keychains fashioned from metal salvaged off a Russian helicopter shot down over Ukrainian soil.

The raffle is stacked with more than just war relics. Backers can also win patches, t-shirts, and game keys, but the studio's ambitions go well beyond swag. **GSC has set a minimum target of $100,000**, with every dollar earmarked for mine clearance operations in the real-world Chornobyl Exclusion Zone — the same irradiated landscape that has inspired its games for decades.

For a studio that weathered full-scale invasion to ship one of 2024's most anticipated titles, the campaign feels less like marketing and more like a mission statement. The Zone has always blurred the line between fiction and harsh reality for GSC. Now, with this fundraiser, they're literally helping to make it safer — one keychain at a time.
