## Final Fantasy VII Revelation gets its release date — and a PS1 throwback

Square Enix has confirmed that Final Fantasy VII Revelation will launch on April 8, 2027, with the announcement coming during a PlayStation State of Play livestream. Alongside the date, the publisher dropped nearly ten minutes of new gameplay footage — and among the biggest reveals was the return of a PS1-style world map, a detail that will thrill longtime fans of the original.

## The PS1-style world map is more than nostalgia bait

The callback to the pre-rendered, overworld-style exploration of the original PlayStation era isn't just a cosmetic nod — it signals that Square Enix is willing to blend classic structure with modern presentation rather than abandon the series' roots entirely. Combined with the scope hinted at by the footage, Revelation looks positioned to be the most ambitious reimagining of the FFVII universe to date.
