September's Humble Choice is here, and it's a stacked one. For the usual **$14.99**, subscribers get eight Steam keys worth over CA$257, headlined by two heavy hitters: *Frostpunk 2* and *Sonic X Shadow Generations*.

## The Headliners Steal the Show

**Frostpunk 2** leads the bundle — 11 bit studios' sequel to the brutal city-building survival game sits at "Mostly Positive" on Steam with nearly 21,000 reviews. It's the kind of game where every policy decision can doom an entire population, and now it costs less than a coffee. **Sonic X Shadow Generations**, meanwhile, holds a 96% "Overwhelmingly Positive" rating from almost 9,000 reviews, making it the highest-rated title in the lineup by a mile.

## Six More Where That Came From

The rest of the pack is nothing to scoff at: *Voidtrain* (a genre-blending rail survival adventure), *Ballionaire*, *Keylocker* (turn-based cyberpunk action), *Pocket Mirror: GoldenerTraum*, *GUNTOUCHABLES* (cel-shaded roguelike shooting), and *Urban Jungle*. The lineup is live now and runs until October 6, so there's a window to decide — but at $15 for this caliber of titles, deliberating might be overthinking it.
