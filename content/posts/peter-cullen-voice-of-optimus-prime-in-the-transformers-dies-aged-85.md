Peter Cullen, the voice actor whose gravely, commanding baritone defined Optimus Prime for generations of Transformers fans, has died at 85. His passing marks the end of one of the most recognizable voices in animation history.

Cullen brought the Autobot leader to life starting with the original 1984 Transformers animated series, and he never really stopped. He reprised the role across nearly every iteration of the franchise — from the Beast Wars era through the live-action Michael Bay films and well into recent video game appearances. His voice was the constant thread that held decades of Transformers storytelling together.

## A Career Far Bigger Than Cybertron

Optimus Prime may be his legacy, but Cullen's résumé stretched far beyond the red-and-blue truck. He voiced characters in Knight Rider, Gremlins, and the 1976 King Kong, among dozens of other projects. In gaming, he appeared in Crystal Crisis and numerous Transformers titles. Few voice actors have ever commanded the kind of lifelong loyalty that Cullen earned simply by showing up and delivering — every single time.

The voice is gone now, but the character — his character — will outlast us all.
