## A day-one drop changes the math

Sony used its September 3 State of Play broadcast to reveal an expanded PlayStation Plus lineup, headlined by Devolver Digital's Mycopunk — a customizable looter-shooter that will launch directly into the Premium and Extra catalog in October 2026. It's a day-one inclusion, meaning subscribers won't need to buy it separately.

Mycopunk's addition is notable because it's one of the more substantial day-one drops PlayStation Plus has offered in recent months. The game's crafting-heavy, build-variety loop and co-op focus give it the kind of replay value that keeps subscribers engaged beyond a single weekend of play.

## What the State of Play showed us

**Sony is clearly investing in making the catalog feel current, not retro.** The September update follows a pattern of pairing surprise drops with more established titles, and the result is a lineup that feels curated rather than padded. For Extra and Premium members, the value proposition just got noticeably stronger.

The full September roster hasn't been completely detailed yet, but with Mycopunk confirmed and additional titles teased, this month shapes up as one of the stronger PlayStation Plus additions of the year. If you've been on the fence about upgrading to Premium, a day-one looter-shooter might be the push you needed.
