## Tokyo Gets Its Own PC Gaming Showcase

PC gaming's marquee showcase is heading east. **Frankie Ward**, the award-winning presenter known for her work across esports and gaming broadcasts, will host the **PC Gaming Show Tokyo Direct** — a dedicated stream promising over 35 titles and a slate of world premieres.

The event signals a continued push by PC Gamer's showcase brand to establish a global footprint beyond its traditional E3-season window. Tokyo, with its dense concentration of Japanese studios and publishers, is a natural staging ground for surprises.

No full lineup has been published yet, but the sheer count of 35-plus games suggests this isn't a sizzle-reel affair. Watch for announcements from both Western and Japanese developers when the stream goes live.
