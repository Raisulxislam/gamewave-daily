## The Blake reveal finally sinks in

All summer, Creative Assembly has been crystal clear about one thing: Alien Isolation 2 will follow a new protagonist named Blake. Yet here I am, only just letting that reality settle. For weeks, I'd been quietly nursing a theory that the sequel would return us to Amanda Ripley — a theory that now looks increasingly like wishful thinking on my part.

## A reluctant goodbye to a favorite idea

The truth is, holding onto that idea was comfortable. Amanda's story felt unfinished, and the idea of stepping away from her felt like leaving a thread dangling. But Creative Assembly has been patient, consistent, and unambiguous all season long. At some point, you have to respect what the studio is telling you.

So this is me, officially, letting go. Alien Isolation 2 will be Blake's story. And honestly, the more I sit with it, the more curious I am about who Blake actually is — and what fresh horrors await in the corridors of the sequel. Maybe the new face in the dark is exactly what the franchise needs.
