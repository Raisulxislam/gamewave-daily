## Stop trying to file it under an existing genre

The fastest way to misread Nodusfall is to compare it to the games everyone keeps reaching for. It's not Elden Ring. It's not Monster Hunter. And despite the obvious studio pedigree, it's not a typical HoYoverse release either. Slotting it into any of those boxes just doesn't hold up.

## A hybrid that refuses to behave

What makes Nodusfall genuinely interesting is that it borrows recognizable DNA from all three — the punishing scale of one, the hunt rituals of another, the slick presentation of the third — and then does something the comparisons don't prepare you for. It's a hybrid that refuses to behave like its obvious predecessors.

That leaves players with something rarer in today's market: a game you have to experience on its own terms before the labels make any sense. The smartest thing to do is to drop the shorthand and let Nodusfall define itself.
