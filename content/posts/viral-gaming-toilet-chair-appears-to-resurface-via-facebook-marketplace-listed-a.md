## The Internet's Favorite Toilet Chair Returns

If you spent any time on gaming TikTok in the last year, you probably remember the Fortnite toilet chair — a fan-made monstrosity that merged a full-size gaming chair with a toilet bowl, complete with themed accents. It became a meme, a legend, and now, apparently, a commodity. The chair has resurfaced on Facebook Marketplace, listed as "used" with an asking price of $500.

The listing has predictably set social media alight again, with users alternately joking about the price, lamenting that someone "actually bought and used" the chair, and wondering whether this is the same unit that was originally posted. At $500 in used condition, it sits in a strange middle ground — too expensive to be a joke purchase, too absurd to be taken seriously.

## When Viral Gag Items Hit the Resale Market

The Fortnite toilet chair is a perfect case study in how internet virality turns novelty items into objects with actual — if bizarre — market value. The chair was never a commercial product; it was a one-off fan creation that caught the internet's attention. Now it has a resale price that rivals a genuine high-end gaming chair.

Whether anyone will actually pay $500 for a used, one-of-a-kind meme chair remains to be seen. But in the world of gaming memorabilia and internet culture, stranger things have sold for more.
