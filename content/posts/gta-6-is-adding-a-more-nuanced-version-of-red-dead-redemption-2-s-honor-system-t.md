Red Dead Redemption 2's honor meter — that sliding moral gauge that rewarded saints and snarled at gunslingers — is getting a successor in the GTA 6 honor system. Rockstar is reportedly building a far more nuanced version of the gauge, and the stated goal is telling: they want Jason and Lucia to feel fallible, not all-powerful.

## A More Nuanced GTA 6 Honor System

In Red Dead Redemption 2, honor was a beautifully blunt instrument: feed and help strangers and the meter climbs toward sainthood; rob and gun down half the county and the world treats you like a plague. It shaped how townspeople spoke and which ending Arthur Morgan earned — yet it rarely kept him from pulling the trigger whenever the itch felt urgent.

## Keeping Power in Check

That bluntness is exactly what the reports say is being sharpened. A more layered honor system weighs each action in context rather than hoisting it onto one grand moral scale, so the same robbery leaves different marks depending on who got hurt, who saw it, and what you did afterward. The payoff isn't just realism; it's consequence. If every two-bit heist left Jason and Lucia no worse off, the whole power fantasy would curdle into god-mode — the exact trap Rockstar claims it wants to avoid.
