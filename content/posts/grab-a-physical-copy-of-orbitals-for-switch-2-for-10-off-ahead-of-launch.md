## A Co-Op Deal Worth Grabbing

If you've been eyeing Orbitals for Switch 2, Walmart is running a preorder deal that cuts $10 off the physical copy ahead of launch. It's a straightforward discount on a co-op title that's been generating steady buzz among Nintendo fans looking for something new to play with friends.

## Physical Copies Aren't Dead Yet

In an industry that's increasingly pushing digital-only, there's something satisfying about snagging a discounted cartridge. The $10 savings on a physical Orbitals preorder is modest, but it's the kind of deal that rewards early adopters who still value having something tangible on their shelf — and a reminder that the Switch 2's physical media ecosystem is very much alive.
