## Morality meter that actually breaks

Grand Theft Auto 6's morality system isn't just a slider — it's a pane of glass. Reports indicate that the game's morality meter will literally "shatter" once a player commits enough mayhem, locking them into an irredeemable criminal trajectory for the rest of the game. There's no going back, no redemption quest to undo the damage.

## Irreversible consequences reshape the GTA 6 experience

This mechanic represents a significant departure from GTA's traditional approach to player morality. Previous entries treated the good-versus-evil spectrum as a reversible pendulum; GTA 6 appears to treat it as a one-time commitment. Cause enough destruction, and the game's world — its story, its characters, its opportunities — permanently shifts around you. It's a system that finally forces players to reckon with the weight of every decision, rather than treating mayhem as cost-free entertainment.
