## From Mice to Game Engines

Razer made its name selling gaming peripherals — mice, keyboards, headsets — and nobody expected the company to pivot into developer tools. Yet at Gamescom 2026, that's exactly what happened. Razer announced the launch of Razer Indie Lab, a program designed to give independent developers access to advanced technology that was previously locked behind AAA budgets.

## What Indie Lab Actually Offers

The program provides indie studios with access to Razer's hardware testing suites, performance optimization tools, and technical consulting. The goal is straightforward: help small teams ship games that look and play like titles from studios ten times their size. Razer is also connecting participating developers with its massive player community for early feedback and visibility.

This isn't charity — it's smart business. By investing in indie success stories, Razer builds loyalty among the developers who increasingly shape gaming culture. If Indie Lab delivers on its promise, it could become one of the most meaningful support programs in the indie space.
