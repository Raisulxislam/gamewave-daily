## Capcom Has Lost Touch With How Frightening Resident Evil Has Become

Hideki Kamiya, the director behind the original *Resident Evil 2*, says Capcom's internal teams have grown "completely numb" to just how terrifying their own games are. Speaking to VGC in an August interview, the PlatinumGames co-founder made it clear he was "one hundred percent serious" about wanting a non-horror accessibility option in future *Resident Evil* titles.

Kamiya has been vocal about this since March 2026, when his current studio Clovers Inc. posted footage of the team playing *Resident Evil Requiem* — the latest entry that brought Leon Kennedy back to Raccoon City. Standing off to the side as a colleague hit a cutscene, Kamiya grumbled that he'd been asking for a "non-scary" mode "forever."

## "Your Games Are Too Scary," Kamiya Told Series Producer Takeuchi

What makes this more than a quirky celebrity complaint is that Kamiya has raised it directly with the people who make the games. He told VGC he confronted series producer Koshi Nakanishi (referred to as Takeuchi-san in the interview) face-to-face: "Your games are too scary!" The producer's response? "What? Seriously?!" Kamiya says he receives codes for every new *Resident Evil*, emails back thanking the team, then admits he can't finish because it's too frightening — and always gets told "come on, it's not that scary."

He even pitched a tongue-in-cheek fix: "brown paper bags on all their heads," pleasant mood lighting in dark rooms, no blood, and zombies replaced by cherry blossom petals. It's funny, but it underscores a real accessibility gap in one of gaming's biggest horror franchises. Capcom, for its part, is moving full speed ahead with a *Code Veronica* remake next year — which probably won't come with Kamiya's preferred paper-bag mode.
