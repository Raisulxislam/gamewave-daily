## Performance Capture Is In the Can

Naughty Dog has confirmed that performance capture on *Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet* — its first entirely new IP in over a decade — has officially wrapped. The announcement, shared on the studio's social channels August 31, is the clearest signal yet that the PlayStation exclusive is deep into production rather than still in the early conceptual stage.

For a studio that has built its reputation on hyper-expressive character work across *The Last of Us* and *Uncharted*, completing performance capture is a genuine milestone. It typically means the broad strokes of the narrative, key action sequences, and character interactions have been acted, recorded, and locked — opening the door to heavy animation polishing, world-building, and systems integration in the months ahead.

## What It Means for the Release Window

Naughty Dog has not offered a release date for *Intergalactic*, and a cautious timeline still seems wise. That said, with Sony's first-party pipeline expected to lean on the title during the current console cycle, an appearance at a future State of Play or PlayStation Showcase feels increasingly likely. Until then, this milestone is as close to concrete news as fans have gotten since the game was first revealed at The Game Awards 2024.
