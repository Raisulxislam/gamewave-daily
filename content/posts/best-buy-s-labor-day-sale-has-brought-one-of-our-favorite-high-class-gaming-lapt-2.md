Best Buy's week-long Labor Day sale has landed on a particularly sweet deal for gamers who want serious horsepower without paying flagship pricing. The ASUS ROG Strix G16, one of GameWave's favorite high-performance gaming laptops, is currently discounted by 24% — a welcome reprieve from the memory-crisis-induced price hikes that have plagued the gaming hardware market this year.

## Performance That Punches Above Its Price

The ROG Strix G16 packs hardware that delivers genuine performance gains for PC gaming, and at this discount it sits comfortably below the $2,000 threshold that keeps a lot of gamers on the sidelines. The sale is part of Best Buy's broader Labor Day promotion, which runs through the holiday weekend, but this particular deal has drawn attention because it directly addresses one of the biggest pain points in the current market — component costs that have pushed even mid-range laptops into premium territory.

## A Timing-Driven Deal Worth Acting On

Memory pricing volatility has been a persistent headache for gaming laptop buyers throughout 2026, and discounts that meaningfully undercut the inflated baseline don't come around often. If you've been waiting for a signal to pull the trigger on a capable gaming machine, this is about as clean a signal as you'll get. The sale won't last forever, and deals with this kind of margin tend to vanish quickly once word gets out.
