The GTA 6 gameplay reveal finally landed, and there is a staggering amount to unpack. Beyond the headline systems, the 26-minute showcase was dense with little tactical maneuvers for pulling off crimes in disguise and shaking the cops — details that hint at how Rockstar wants players to actually think on the job.

## Crimes in disguise

A recurring thread in the reveal is preparation: an outfit change, swapping out a vehicle, wiping the heat off your profile before it sticks. Rockstar is pushing players to treat scoring and escaping as a craft rather than a blunt instrument.

## Escaping the cops

Police awareness is more granular now. Change how you look, ditch the car, split up as a duo — every small decision matters when the chase kicks off. It's a lighter, smarter GTA 6 than we've ever had, and these tactics are the roadmap.
