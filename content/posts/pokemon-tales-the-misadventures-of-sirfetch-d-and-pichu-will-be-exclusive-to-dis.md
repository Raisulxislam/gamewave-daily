## A stop-motion surprise for Pokemon fans

The Pokemon franchise is heading to streaming in a fresh form. Pokemon Tales: The Misadventures of Sirfetch'd and Pichu, an upcoming stop-motion series, will stream exclusively on Disney+ and DisneyXD.

The announcement landed during a special showcase at PokémonXP, where fans got their first official look at the series' charming, tactile animation style.

## A Disney home for two unlikely stars

Landing a dedicated streaming home on Disney+ marks a notable move for the brand — and gives Sirfetch'd and Pichu, a pairing you wouldn't immediately put together, their own cozy corner of the Pokemon universe. With stop-motion bringing a distinctly handcrafted feel to the pocket-monster world, this is one series collectors and casual fans alike may want to keep an eye on.
