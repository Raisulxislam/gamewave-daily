The Xbox Series X25 anniversary console is officially up for pre-order, and it took about five minutes for the inevitable to happen: scalpers are now listing it for thousands on eBay.

It's the same dance we've seen with every limited run. Units sell out fast, bots snatch up the stock, and the markups on resale markets climb into the four figures before most real fans even get a look in.

## The X25 Pre-Order Feeding Frenzy

As with past anniversary editions, the X25 is a collector's item dressed in nostalgia — exactly the kind of hardware scalpers know they can flip. Within hours of pre-orders opening, listings appeared with price tags well above retail.

## What It Means for Genuine Fans

For the people this console was actually made for, it's a frustrating repeat of history. If you're determined to pick one up, your best bet is acting the second pre-orders go live and steering well clear of the resale markup. The scalpers are already waiting — but so, fairly, are the fans.
