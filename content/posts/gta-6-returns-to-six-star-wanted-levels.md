It turns out the five-star cap quietly took something from Grand Theft Auto. GTA 6 is restoring the six-star wanted level, bringing back the maximum-heat chases that defined Rockstar's open worlds for years before GTA 5 trimmed the ceiling down to five.

## Why Six-Star Heat Matters

The five-star system made escapes more forgiving and, in equal measure, less gloriously absurd. With six stars back on the table, GTA 6 is promising the kind of frantic, kitchen-sink pursuit that used to be a rite of passage — heat climbing past anything the series has thrown recently.

## A Small Return That Feels Huge

Wanted-level detail has always been a point of pride in the GTA community, and the climb back to six is being treated as a quiet victory for the old-school crowd. **It's a single number, but in a series this storied, that number carries the whole vibe — and it says Rockstar is listening to its roots.**
