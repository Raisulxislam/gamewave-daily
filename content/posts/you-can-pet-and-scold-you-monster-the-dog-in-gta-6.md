Grand Theft Auto 6 made its splashy debut on Netflix today, and buried inside the opening moments was confirmation of something wholesome for a series that usually trades in chaos: dogs. Very good boys, by the sound of things — and crucially, you can actually interact with them.

## A Softer Side of Los Santos

Games rarely dwell on the simple pleasure of petting a dog, but the reveal suggests the studio wants players to slow down between the car chases. The animals are presented as interactive presences in the world rather than background decoration, a small but meaningful step toward making the city feel more alive.

## Pet, or Scold — the Choice Is Yours

The headline hook, though, is that interaction cuts both ways. You can pet the dog — or, if you're feeling particularly villainous (you monster), you can scold it too. Whether that opens any gameplay doors or is purely for the roleplayers among us remains to be seen. Either way, it's a welcome reminder that even Rockstar's crime epic has room for a little tenderness between the mayhem.
