## The Ultra-Premium Handheld Gets a Price Drop

If you've been eyeing the Asus ROG Xbox Ally X but wincing at the price tag, now might be the time to pull the trigger. The flagship handheld — powered by AMD's Ryzen Z2 Extreme processor, widely regarded as the most powerful chip in any Windows-based gaming handheld — has dropped to $899.99, making it the least expensive entry in the ultra-premium handheld gaming category.

That's still serious money for a portable device, but context matters here. The ROG Xbox Ally X competes in a space where rivals regularly clear $1,000, and its combination of raw processing power, display quality, and Xbox ecosystem integration gives it a genuine edge.

## What You Get for the Money

At $899.99, you're getting the most capable Windows gaming handheld currently available. The Ryzen Z2 Extreme delivers desktop-class performance in a form factor you can carry in a backpack, and the Xbox integration means seamless access to Game Pass and the Microsoft library without fiddling with third-party launchers.

Is it cheap? Absolutely not. But in a market where the Steam Deck OLED sits at a lower price point with less power, and competitors like the Legion Go S push well past the $1,000 mark, the ROG Xbox Ally X at sub-$900 starts to look like the smart money pick for players who want maximum performance without paying the absolute maximum price.
