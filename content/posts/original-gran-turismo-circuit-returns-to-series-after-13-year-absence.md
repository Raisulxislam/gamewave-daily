Polyphony Digital has confirmed that **Autumn Ring** — one of Gran Turismo's most iconic original circuits — will return in the next major update for **Gran Turismo 7**. It's been thirteen years since the fictional countryside track last appeared in the franchise, and its comeback is easily the headline grabber in a packed content drop.

## A Track That Defined an Era

First introduced in Gran Turismo 4, Autumn Ring became a fan favourite for its sweeping elevation changes and tight chicane-heavy layout. It was conspicuously absent throughout the PS4 era, and fans had long speculated whether it would ever resurface. The answer, it turns out, was yes — and the timing feels deliberate.

## Sportsland Sugo Makes Its Series Debut

Alongside Autumn Ring, the update marks **Sportsland Sugo**'s first-ever appearance in a Gran Turismo game. The real-life Japanese circuit, nestled in the mountains of Miyagi Prefecture, has hosted everything from Super GT to junior formula series, and it's a natural fit for the franchise's commitment to authentic Japanese motorsport venues. The drop also includes more than a dozen new cars and a range of additional features yet to be fully detailed.

With Gran Turismo 7 continuing to receive significant post-launch support well into 2026, Polyphony Digital shows no sign of slowing down — and Autumn Ring's return feels like a love letter to long-time series veterans.
