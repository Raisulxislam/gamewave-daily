Onimusha: Way of the Sword is back, and it comes with the difficulty options you would expect from a sword-fighting revenge tale — including a proper hard mode.

## Difficulty Settings, Explained

Capcom's action title gives players a range of difficulty settings to choose from when they start. The headline addition for the hardcore crowd is a hard mode designed to punish careless swings and reward genuine mastery of the blade.

## The Catch? It's Locked

Here's the twist: the hardest difficulty isn't waiting at the start screen. You have to earn your way in, progressing through the game's challenge before the brutal mode unlocks. It's a classic design choice — one that keeps newcomers from bouncing into a wall, while giving returning players a goal to grind toward.

For series veterans craving the old-school hurt, the message is simple: pick up the sword, and prove you deserve the hard mode.
