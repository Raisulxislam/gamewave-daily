Mrs. Doubtfire is officially getting the 4K treatment, and the release lands September 29. For a generation that grew up quoting Robin Williams's frantic cross-dressing parent, this is the kind of upgrade that matters — not just a resolution bump, but a proper restoration of a film that deserved better than the DVD-era transfers it's been stuck with.

The 4K release promises a new scan from the original camera negative, with HDR grading that should bring warmth and texture to the San Francisco setting. No word yet on whether the infamous deleted scenes — including the significantly darker ending that test audiences rejected — will make the cut.

## Where to Preorder

Major retailers including Amazon, Best Buy, and Zavvi are stocking both standard and limited-edition SteelBook versions. The SteelBook, as always, will likely sell through fast if past 4K catalog releases are anything to go by. **If you're a physical-media collector, preorder the SteelBook now rather than hoping it'll still be there on release day.**

## What's in the Bonus Features

The release includes retrospective documentary segments, audio commentary from the surviving cast and crew, and a breakdown of the practical makeup effects that turned Williams into a convincing elderly Scottish nanny. It's a solid package for a film that's been under-served by home video for years.
