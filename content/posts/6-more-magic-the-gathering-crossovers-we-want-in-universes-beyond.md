Magic: The Gathering's Universes Beyond line has gone from odd experiment to certified money-printer, pulling franchises like Fallout and Final Fantasy onto the table. At this rate, the dream Universes Beyond crossovers basically write themselves — and fans already have a very clear shortlist.

## The Universes Beyond crossovers that feel inevitable

First stop on any credible wishlist: Narnia. The wardrobe, Aslan, the White Witch — the ingredients are basically pre-packaged for a card set. Then comes The Cosmere, where Brandon Sanderson builds worlds that collide with each other by design, making it a near-perfect fit for a multiverse card game. And The Elder Scrolls would practically beg for a Commander preconstructed wave, from Skyrim's dragon shouts to Morrowind's mushroom towers.

## Crossover fever has a flip side

Some purists will roll their eyes — and honestly, part of that instinct is fair. Universes Beyond can start to feel like it's eating the very game it's supposed to be a guest star in. But the line has also proven it can pull in players who would never have walked through Magic's door otherwise. As long as the picks are made with care, the fans' shortlist is one worth paying attention to.
