Sony has officially announced two limited-edition GTA 6-themed DualSense controllers, giving PlayStation 5 owners a stylish way to celebrate the upcoming release. The controllers were unveiled during the recent State of Play broadcast, featuring unique designs that capture the game's vibrant Miami-inspired aesthetic.

## DualSense Controllers Get GTA 6 Makeover

The first controller sports a neon-lit Vice City color scheme, with gradient blues and pinks that evoke the game's sunset-soaked streets. The second design showcases a more subdued, urban look, incorporating graffiti-style artwork and the iconic GTA 6 logo.

## Availability and Pricing

Both limited-edition controllers will be available for pre-order starting next month, with a release date set just ahead of GTA 6's launch. Pricing has not been officially confirmed, but similar special-edition DualSense controllers have typically retailed around $79.99.

Sony previously released limited-edition DualSense controllers for God of War Ragnarok and Spider-Man 2, demonstrating the company's commitment to celebrating major PlayStation releases with unique hardware. The GTA 6 controllers continue this tradition, offering fans a tangible piece of the game's world.
