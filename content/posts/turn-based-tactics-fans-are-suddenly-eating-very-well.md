For turn-based-tactics fans, the drought has been real. The XCOM series has effectively sat in battery for years, and genuinely big-budget games in the genre have been frustratingly scarce — anyone craving squad-scale, grid-savvy strategy has had to scavenge through indies or revisit the classics.

## A Genre Rediscovered

Now, suddenly, that sparseness is reversing. A surge of new releases — and long-rumored projects finally surfacing — means fans who were starved for options are, for the first time in a long while, eating very well indeed.

## Worth the Wait

The shift feels overdue. The appetite was always there; it was the supply that had gone missing. With a crowded pipeline forming, the question is no longer whether turn-based-tactics will get its moment again, but which of these stacked releases will actually stick the landing. For a niche that's waited this long, that embarrassment of riches is its own kind of victory.
