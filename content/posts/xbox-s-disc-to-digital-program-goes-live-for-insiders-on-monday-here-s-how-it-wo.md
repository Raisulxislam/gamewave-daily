## Rolling Out to Xbox Insiders First

Microsoft is taking a cautious approach to its most collector-friendly feature yet. The disc-to-digital program launches Monday for Xbox Insider members, letting players register a physical disc and receive a matching digital licence in their account. The company claims support for "thousands" of titles from day one, though the exact catalogue remains unconfirmed.

## The Mechanics: Simple, But With Caveats

The process itself appears straightforward: insert a supported disc, follow the on-screen prompt to link it digitally, and the title appears in your library going forward. It's the kind of quality-of-life improvement that sounds obvious in hindsight — and one that likely took significant negotiation with publishers who have long viewed physical and digital as separate revenue streams.

## What We Still Don't Know

The elephant in the room remains unaddressed: Microsoft has said nothing about the long-term future of physical Xbox games. Will new releases continue to ship on disc? Will the programme expand beyond Insiders? For now, the answers are cautiously optimistic at best. The feature is a genuine win for anyone who still buys discs, but whether it signals a durable commitment or a graceful sunset for physical media is anyone's guess.
