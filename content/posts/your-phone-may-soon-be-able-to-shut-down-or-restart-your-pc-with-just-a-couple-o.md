Your next PC power button might live in your pocket. Hidden code uncovered in the latest builds suggests that Link to Windows — the bridge between Android phones and Windows PCs — is about to add a genuinely handy trick: letting you shut down, restart, or put your PC to sleep straight from your phone.

## Power, From Your Pocket

A couple of taps, and your machine goes dark from across the room. The feature would slot neatly into the existing Link to Windows toolkit, which already lets you manage files, mirror notifications, and answer calls without touching the desktop. Being able to reach the power state remotely is a small but welcome convenience for anyone who's ever left a download running or wanted to kill a machine without getting up.

## Not Quite Official Yet

The clues are firmly in the hidden-code territory for now, so there's no launch date and no guarantee it ships in its current form. But the direction is clear: Windows wants your phone to feel like a remote control for your entire setup. If it lands, expect the predictable jokes about turning off your partner's PC mid-gaming to follow almost immediately.
