October wouldn't feel right without a spooky game or two, and Hellraiser: Revival is making a strong case to be the season's freakiest arrival. This interactive plunge into the nightmarish fiction of author Clive Barker is shaping up to be genuinely terrifying — and, by its own pitch, unapologetically hungry — reaching well beyond the familiar parade of cenobites and gore.

## Hellraiser: Revival Goes Beyond the Puzzle Box

What we've learned so far points to a team reaching for mood and mythology at least as hard as they reach for shock value. That means more than blood-slicked corridors and the franchise's infamous puzzle box; the game is said to anchor itself in the uneasy mystique that made the material endure — forbidden doors, whispered contracts, pleasure twisted into pain. Expect the uncomfortable and the seductive to share the same hallway.

## An Experience That Gets Under the Skin

If the ambition holds, Hellraiser: Revival will be remembered less for what it shows than for what it makes you feel — a horror story that wants to crawl under your skin, not just splatter across it. October just found itself a genuinely interesting monster.
