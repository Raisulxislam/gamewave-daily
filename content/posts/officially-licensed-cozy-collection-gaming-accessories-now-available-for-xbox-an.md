Xbox has partnered with 8BitDo, SteelSeries, and KontrolFreek to launch the **Cozy Collection**, a range of officially licensed accessories designed around one simple idea: gaming should feel comfortable. The lineup spans controllers, headsets, and thumbstick grips, all tuned for longer sessions where ergonomics matter more than RGB lighting.

## Comfort-First, Brand-Second

The standout choice here is the emphasis on soft-touch materials and muted color palettes instead of the aggressive angular designs that dominate most gaming peripherals. It is a surprisingly smart positioning move — the "cozy gaming" trend has exploded on social media, and Xbox is clearly paying attention.

## Available Now Across Xbox and PC

Everything in the Cozy Collection works on Xbox consoles and PC, so you are not locked into a single ecosystem. For players who spend hours in games like Stardew Valley or PowerWash Simulator, the pitch writes itself.
