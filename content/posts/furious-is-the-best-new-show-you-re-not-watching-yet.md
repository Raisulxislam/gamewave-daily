## Furious is the Hulu crime thriller nobody's talking about — yet

Emmy Rossum and Lola Petticrew anchor Hulu's eight-episode crime thriller Furious, which wrapped its first season this week — and it's an absolute shame if you haven't been paying attention. Creator-driven crime television doesn't get much tighter than this: every episode lands with the precision of a much longer series, and the finale sealed it as one of the year's most satisfying new shows.

## The finale cemented Furious as a must-watch

Furious doesn't reinvent the crime genre, but it executes within it with a confidence that most new series simply don't have. Rossum and Petticrew share an immediate, combustible chemistry that elevates every scene they're in, and the writing refuses to waste a single minute across all eight episodes. If you've been looking for a reason to justify a Hulu subscription this year, this is it.
