## Scalebound Rises From the Grave

One of gaming's most famous cancelled projects just got a second chance at life — if the mood strikes one particular executive. Bayonetta creator Hideki Kamiya has publicly signaled to Xbox CEO Asha Sharma that he is ready to make Scalebound whenever she is.

That's the kind of one-line teardown that sends the internet into a frenzy, and for good reason. Scalebound was cancelled in early 2017 after years of troubled development, a game that once promised players a dragon-mounted, PlatinumGames-flavoured action epic. Its death has been mourned by fans ever since as one of the great wh-ifs of the generation.

## A Message That Broke the Internet

Kamiya's public readiness matters because it reframes the whole saga. The original collapse was a messy, mutually embarrassing affair, with both sides hinting at different blame. That a creative of Kamiya's stature would come out and say the door is open — unprompted, in public — suggests the wounds may have finally healed.

Of course, "when she is" is doing a lot of work in that sentence. Xbox's stance has always been that Scalebound was a project it couldn't deliver on in time, and neither side has to do anything. But in an industry where revivals are suddenly fashionable — and where Xbox is hungrier than ever for exclusive firepower against Sony's catalog — the message lands with real weight.

For the fans who kept the dream alive for nearly a decade, the ball is now squarely in Xbox's court. Kamiya has said his piece. The question is whether Asha Sharma calls his bluff.
