Preorders for Final Fantasy VII Revelation went live today, and Square Enix is offering the franchise's now-customary multi-edition spread. The base game launches April 8, 2027, across PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam and the Epic Games Store.

## Edition Breakdown: Standard to Collector's

The Standard Edition includes the base game and whatever day-one bonus Square Enix has bundled in. The Deluxe and Collector's tiers add physical and digital extras — art books, soundtrack access, and in-game cosmetic items. As with most modern AAA preorders, the value proposition depends heavily on how much you care about non-gameplay trinkets.

## Is the Premium Price Worth It?

Square Enix has historically leaned hard on collector's editions to drive early revenue, and Revelation appears to continue that pattern. If you're a day-one buyer regardless, locking in a preorder secures your copy and any early-access bonuses. If you're on the fence, waiting for review scores before committing your wallet is never the worst move — especially with a game this ambitious.
