## Naughty Dog Isn't Walking Away From The Last of Us

Neil Druckmann has made it clear: Naughty Dog is not done with The Last of Us. In recent comments, the studio head confirmed that the developer still has ambitions for the franchise on the game side — even as the HBO adaptation races ahead without them.

That is a meaningful statement from a studio that has historically been cautious about sequels and spin-offs. If Druckmann is publicly signaling that more is coming, it is worth paying attention.

## But the HBO Show Is Someone Else's Fight Now

Here is the twist: Druckmann also confirmed that neither he nor anyone at Naughty Dog is involved with the third season of HBO's The Last of Us. That season is being steered entirely by the show's own creative team, which has already demonstrated it is willing to diverge significantly from the source material.

It is a rare and somewhat unusual arrangement — a game studio stepping back from its own IP's television life while simultaneously promising that the game side is alive and well. For fans, it means two separate but parallel futures for The Last of Us, each with its own creative stewards.
