## A High-Profile Lawsuit Hits a High-Profile Series

Ed Brubaker — the celebrated comic-book writer who co-created Marvel's Winter Soldier and is currently showrunning Prime Video's adaptation of his own series Criminal — is now facing a lawsuit from a former production assistant. The suit, filed this week, alleges that Brubaker fostered a hostile work environment during production of the show.\n## The Broader Fallout for Criminal

The allegations are serious enough to cast a shadow over one of Prime Video's most anticipated genre series. Criminal, based on Brubaker and Sean Phillips' acclaimed noir comic, has been positioned as a prestige crime drama. A lawsuit targeting its showrunner — one of the creative forces most closely identified with the source material — raises immediate questions about the production's internal culture and the show's path forward.

**It's worth noting that Brubaker is not just any showrunner.** He is one of the most respected voices in modern crime comics, and the Winter Soldier character he co-created with artist Steve Epting has become a cornerstone of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. This makes the lawsuit not merely a workplace dispute but a story with ripple effects across the entertainment industry.
