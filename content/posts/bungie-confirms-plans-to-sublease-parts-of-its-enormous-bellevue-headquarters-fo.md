Bungie has confirmed plans to sublease a significant portion of its sprawling Bellevue, Washington headquarters — 210,984 square feet, to be exact — following the mass layoffs that rocked the studio earlier this year. In a statement, the company characterized the move as consistent with its ongoing shift toward a "digital first" operating model, but the optics are hard to ignore: a studio downsizing its physical footprint immediately after cutting a large chunk of its workforce tells a very specific story about where Bungie thinks its future lies.

## "Digital First" Means Something Real Here

The phrase "digital first" has become a catch-all for studios looking to justify reduced office space, but in Bungie's case, there's a concrete operational logic behind it. The studio's development pipeline has been increasingly distributed, with remote and hybrid work becoming standard across the industry. Subleasing 210,000 square feet — a substantial chunk of any office campus — signals that Bungie isn't planning to backfill those desks anytime soon. The company is betting that a smaller physical footprint, paired with a more flexible workforce, is how it stays competitive.

## The Bigger Picture for Bungie's Future

These sublease plans arrive at a moment when Bungie's trajectory is under intense scrutiny. The layoffs, the real estate downsizing, and the quiet pivot to a leaner operating model all point to a studio in genuine transition. Whether that transition leads to renewed creative focus or further contraction remains to be seen, but the decisions being made now — about space, about headcount, about how work actually gets done — will define Bungie for the next several years.
