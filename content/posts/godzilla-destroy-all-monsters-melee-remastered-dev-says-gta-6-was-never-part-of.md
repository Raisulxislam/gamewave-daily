## A Kaiju-Sized Challenge, Ignored

Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered is heading for a November launch — the same month as Grand Theft Auto 6, arguably the most anticipated game in the industry's history. For most developers, that would be cause for serious reconsideration. For the team behind the arena brawler, though, it apparently wasn't even a factor. "It was never part of our thinking," the studio said when asked about the overlap.

## Confidence, or Just Knowing Your Lane?

There's something refreshing about a developer that refuses to play the calendar-avoidance game. Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered isn't trying to be GTA 6 — it's a faithful revival of a beloved early-2000s arena fighter, targeting a specific audience that's been waiting for exactly this kind of love letter. Going head-to-head with Rockstar might look reckless on paper, but niche passion projects have survived worse. The real question isn't whether it can compete with GTA 6, but whether it can find its people.
