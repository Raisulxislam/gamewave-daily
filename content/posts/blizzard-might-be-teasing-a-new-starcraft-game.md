## The Glitch Heard Round Koprulu

Blizzard's official StarCraft website is behaving strangely. Elements flicker. Images cut out at random. Text blocks jump and vanish mid-sentence. The page still nominally promotes StarCraft Remastered — the 2017 revival of the classic 1998 RTS — but the deliberate visual corruption has set the community buzzing.

**This doesn't look like a broken CDN.** Studio websites don't accidentally develop cinematic glitch effects. The flickering pattern reads as a carefully staged teaser, the kind Blizzard has used before to signal incoming announcements.

## Reading the Signals

BlizzCon 2026 is on the horizon, and the timing is too neat to dismiss. A brand-new StarCraft title — whether a sequel, a reboot, or something structurally different — would be the kind of tentpole reveal that justifies an entire convention keynote. For now, all we have is a haunted website and a very suspicious silence from Irvine.
