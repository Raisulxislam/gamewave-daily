## A Promising Delay, Not a Cancellation

If you were worried that Nvidia's next-generation upscaling tech might leave your RTX 40 series card behind, take a breath. An Nvidia employee posted on the company's official forums confirming that DLSS 5 will be expanded to include older RTX 40 series graphics cards — it's a matter of when, not if.

## What This Means for PC Gamers

DLSS, or Deep Learning Super Sampling, has been one of Nvidia's biggest competitive advantages in the GPU wars. Each generation has brought meaningful improvements in image quality and performance, and DLSS 5 promises to push that further. For owners of RTX 40 series hardware, the confirmation is welcome news, even if the timeline remains vague. Nvidia has not shared a specific release window for the expansion.

**Our take: backward compatibility has never been Nvidia's strongest instinct — the company would much rather you buy the newest card.** That it is bothering to confirm support for last-generation hardware at all suggests the RTX 40 install base is simply too large to ignore. Expect the wait to be measured in months, not weeks.
