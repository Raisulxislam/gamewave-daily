If you've ever built a perfect roller coaster in **Planet Coaster** or managed a dinosaur park in *Jurassic World Evolution*, you already know what Frontier Developments does best: letting players build dream worlds and then watch chaos unfold inside them. Now Frontier is taking that talent to the biggest playground in entertainment — Disney.

The UK-based studio has announced a new partnership with Disney for an unannounced title, and the gaming community's reaction has been immediate and electric. Details are still scarce — we don't know the genre, the timeline, or which Disney IP is involved — but the mere pairing has people dreaming of possibilities that Frontier's management-sim DNA could unlock inside Disney's universe of properties.

Think about it. Frontier has spent years refining the art of player-driven theme park creation. Disney *is* theme parks. The logical overlap is almost too clean to ignore. A full-featured Disney park management sim, a *Star Wars* colony builder, a Marvel superhero headquarters sim — the concept space is massive, and Frontier is one of the few studios on Earth with the track record to pull any of it off credibly.

No release window has been shared, but this is absolutely a partnership worth watching closely.
