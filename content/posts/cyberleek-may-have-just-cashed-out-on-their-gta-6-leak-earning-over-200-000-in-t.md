Some GTA 6 leaks end in hype; this one may have ended in a payday. Cyberleek, the account behind a recent Grand Theft Auto 6 leak, reportedly cashed out for more than $200,000 by attaching a crypto token to the leak's notoriety — then letting the token do what hype tokens always do.

## The GTA 6 leak money machine, explained

The formula is ugly but simple. Turn a leak into attention, attach a token to that attention, and sell the top before the attention cools. Cyberleek's exit looks like a textbook case — the only genuine surprise is how openly the whole thing unfolded.

## A predictable ending, an avoidable loss

Nobody should lose sleep over the leaker's bag; the real casualties are buyers who traded real money for a meme built on someone else's scoop. If this saga teaches anything, it's that hype is a currency — and in crypto, someone is always cashing it out before you do.
