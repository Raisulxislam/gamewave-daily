For two decades, Grand Theft Auto has let you stroll the boulevard with an arsenal on your shoulders while the only person raising an eyebrow was nobody. That carefree era appears to be ending: the GTA 6 NPCs are reportedly being taught to notice when you walk around openly armed, and to treat you accordingly.

## GTA 6 NPCs Notice the Gun in the Room

Franchise veterans will probably feel a twinge of “finally.” Marching into a convenience store with a rifle hanging off your back and being greeted like a regular customer has always been one of the series' silliest contradictions — a wanted man passing unnoticed through a city full of eyes. Giving the street a self-preservation instinct is long overdue.

## A World That Fights Back

None of this means GTA 6 is becoming a stealth sandbox. But a pedestrian who clocks your sidearm shouldn't keep standing around like a mannequin — the natural rhythm of such a system is suspicion, distance, and the kind of wide berth that makes a city feel alive. It's a small change on paper and a surprisingly large one in practice: being armed in public becomes a decision with consequences, not a background habit. GTA 6 may still let you be the terror of the streets — it just plans to make sure the streets notice the danger first.
