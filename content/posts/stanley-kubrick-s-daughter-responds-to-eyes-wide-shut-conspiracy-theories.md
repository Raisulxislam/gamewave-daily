Vivian Kubrick, daughter of the legendary filmmaker Stanley Kubrick, has publicly addressed one of cinema's most persistent conspiracy theories. For over two decades, fans have speculated that crucial scenes were removed from Eyes Wide Shut following Kubrick's death in March 1999 — just weeks before the film's release.

## Separating Fact from Myth

Vivian's statement directly contradicts the long-held claim that the studio interfered with the final cut. She confirmed that the version audiences saw was Kubrick's own, effectively closing the door on theories that have fueled online forums and documentary deep dives since the early 2000s.

## Why This Theory Refused to Die

Eyes Wide Shut was always going to invite speculation. Its cryptic tone, sexually charged imagery, and Kubrick's perfectionist reputation created fertile ground for rumors. Now, with a family member speaking on the record, the conspiracy may finally lose its most compelling hook.
