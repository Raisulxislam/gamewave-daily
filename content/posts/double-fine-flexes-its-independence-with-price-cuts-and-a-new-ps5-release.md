## Double Fine Goes Independent — And Immediately Makes Moves

Double Fine Productions has wasted no time asserting its independence. After parting ways with Microsoft's ownership, the studio used Sony's September State of Play to announce that its surreal adventure game *Keeper* is shadow-dropping on PS5 — available immediately, no advance hype cycle required. Simultaneously, Double Fine slashed prices across its catalog, a clear signal that the studio sees its newfound freedom as an opportunity to reconnect with players on its own terms.

**This is what independence looks like when a studio actually commits to it.** Rather than quietly fading into the background after losing a major publisher, Double Fine is making aggressive, player-friendly decisions that remind everyone why Tim Schafer's studio has remained culturally relevant for over two decades.

## Keeper's PS5 Launch Comes With Meaningful Updates

*Keeper*'s surprise PS5 release isn't just a platform port. Double Fine confirmed that the acclaimed adventure game arrives with intriguing new updates, suggesting the studio has been iterating on the experience since its original launch. The details on what those updates entail remain light, but the studio's willingness to pair a surprise drop with fresh content shows a playbook that prioritizes delight over marketing logistics.

The price cuts extend beyond *Keeper*, covering Double Fine's broader catalog. For a studio that built its reputation on creative risk-taking, the combination of aggressive pricing, surprise releases, and cross-platform availability reads as a mission statement: Double Fine is independent now, and it intends to play like it.
