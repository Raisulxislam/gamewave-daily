Mark your calendar: Nintendo is raising the price of the Switch 2 by $50 starting September 1, pushing the base console to $500. If you've been on the fence, this weekend is effectively your last window to grab one at the current rate.

The price hike applies to the standard Switch 2 console, though Nintendo has not detailed whether bundled SKUs or accessories will see similar adjustments. The company has not publicly explained the reasoning behind the increase, leaving fans to speculate about supply chain pressures, tariff impacts, or simply the economics of launching a new-generation console in 2026.

## $500 Changes the Conversation

At $450, the Switch 2 sat in a competitive sweet spot against the PS5 and Xbox Series X. Crossing the $500 threshold shifts the value proposition considerably — especially for families and casual buyers who were drawn to the Switch line's traditionally accessible pricing.

Nintendo has moved tens of millions of units at launch price across previous generations, and a $50 increase at this stage is unusual. It's a signal that Nintendo either sees enough demand to absorb the hike, or that the margins on the hardware were thinner than the company would like.
