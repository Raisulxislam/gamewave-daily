Sony just quietly turned your PlayStation 5 into a streaming box. Without any prior buildup, the company has launched a free ad-supported streaming TV service — commonly known as FAST — on PS5, giving players access to live and on-demand movies and TV shows at no cost, so long as they're willing to sit through commercials.

The service channels are available directly through the PS5's media interface, and the library includes a mix of movies, series, and linear TV-style channels that play content on a schedule. It's a model that's already proven enormously popular on smart TVs and streaming sticks, and Sony is clearly betting that console owners will find it equally appealing.

## Why This Makes More Sense Than You'd Think

PlayStation has been quietly building out its media capabilities for years, and this move slots neatly into a broader industry push toward ad-supported tiers. Netflix, Disney+, and Amazon have all launched their own FAST or ad-subsidized options — Sony is just cutting out the subscription middleman by putting it directly on the hardware.

For gamers who already spend hours on the couch with a DualSense in hand, the pitch is simple: free content, minimal effort, and zero additional monthly bills.
