## Winter Comes to the West End

Westeros is trading screens for the stage. The Game of Thrones stage play The Mad King is coming to London in 2027, following a sold-out run at the Royal Shakespeare Company. The show, drawn from the mind of George R.R. Martin, takes audiences deep into the fractured mind of the man who eventually lights the realm on fire.

It's a bold bet on the franchise's future in a post-TV world. Having blistered through its RSC engagement, the production is now set to open in the capital, where it will face the notoriously tough London theatre crowd.

## A Daenerys of a Different Kind

This is the second Game of Thrones stage production to make the leap from page to play, and the choice of subject matter is telling. The Mad King's story — a slow, tragic slide into paranoia — is the kind of intimate psychological drama that the big-budget small screen never quite had time for, and theatre seems like the right home for it.

The move to London signals real confidence. West End transfers don't happen on a whim; they're a commercial verdict on a show's quality. George R.R. Martin's world has proven it can hold audiences for decades, and the stage seems to be the next chapter of that long, strange saga. For fans, all that's left is to mark 2027 on the calendar — and hope there are enough tickets to go round.
