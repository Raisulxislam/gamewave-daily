## Charting the Unknown Brings Home-Building to Crimson Desert

Pearl Abyss has confirmed that Crimson Desert's first major expansion, *Charting the Unknown*, is arriving in October — and it's bringing an entirely unexpected twist to the open-world action RPG. During Sony's September State of Play, the studio revealed that the DLC leans heavily into settlement building, crafting, and home management, drawing unmistakable inspiration from life-simulation heavyweights like The Sims.

**The expansion doesn't just bolt on a house — it layers a full domestic system onto the combat-driven world.** Players will be able to construct and customize living spaces, furnish rooms, and nurture relationships with NPCs in a way that goes well beyond the typical RPG side quest. It's a bold swing for a game that already juggles exploration, real-time combat, and narrative-driven quests.

## A Roguelite Survival World That Demands Adaptation

The Sims parallels aren't purely cosmetic. Pearl Abyss is adding roguelite survival mechanics to Charting the Unknown, where players must adapt their shelters and strategies to shifting environmental threats. The expansion promises procedurally generated challenges that reshape how players approach both combat and settlement management, blending the tension of survival gameplay with the satisfaction of building something permanent.

For an open-world RPG that already earned praise for its scale and ambition, the decision to weave in home-building signals Pearl Abyss's willingness to push Crimson Desert further into uncharted territory — quite literally.
