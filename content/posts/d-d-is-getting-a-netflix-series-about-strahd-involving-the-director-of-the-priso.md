## Alfonso Cuarón Takes on Ravenloft

Here's a crossover nobody saw coming. Netflix has given the green light to a series based on Curse of Strahd, the beloved Dungeons & Dragons horror module set in the gothic nightmare of Ravenloft. And the kicker: Alfonso Cuarón — the director behind The Prisoner of Azkaban, Children of Men, and Roma — is attached to direct.

## Why This Particular Pairing Might Click

Curse of Strahd is widely regarded as the best module in D&D's modern catalogue. Its tale of a cursed vampire lord, a haunted castle, and a party of adventurers trapped in a land of eternal darkness has captivated tabletop groups for over a decade. Translating that experience to screen has always felt like a natural fit — but the quality of the adaptation would hinge entirely on who held the reins. Putting Cuarón behind the camera changes the equation entirely.

**This is a director who turned a Harry Potter film into one of the franchise's best entries and made a post-apocalyptic drama that critics still cite as a masterpiece.** If anyone can take Strahd von Zarovich from the tabletop to prestige television without losing the atmosphere that makes the module special, Cuarón is as good a bet as any.
