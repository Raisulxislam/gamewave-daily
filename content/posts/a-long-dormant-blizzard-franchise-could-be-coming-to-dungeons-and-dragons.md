## A New Crossover on the Horizon

Dungeons & Dragons has built a steady track record of pulling outside IPs into its tabletop universe, and the next one might come from Blizzard's own catalog. Rumblings suggest a **long-dormant Blizzard franchise** is being discussed for a D&D crossover — one separate from the World of Warcraft collaboration that Wizards of the Coast already confirmed.

## Why This Matters for Both Sides

The WoW × D&D partnership showed that high-fantasy crossovers can sell sourcebooks and pull tabletop veterans into Blizzard's orbit. A second collaboration — especially one tied to a franchise Blizzard hasn't touched in years — would signal that WotC sees crossover D&D products as a genuine growth pillar, not a one-off novelty.

Neither Blizzard nor Wizards of the Coast has officially named the franchise in question, so salt is advised. But if the pattern holds, expect an announcement tied to a major gaming or tabletop event before the year is out.
