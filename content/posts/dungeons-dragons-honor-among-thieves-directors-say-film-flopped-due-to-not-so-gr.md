## When the Dice Roll Against You

John Francis Daley and Jonathan Goldstein, the directing duo behind Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves, are finally speaking candidly about why their 2023 fantasy adventure underperformed at the box office. Their verdict? **Terrible timing.**

The film opened in March 2023, just weeks before The Super Mario Bros. Movie steamrolled into theaters and dominated the family-adventure space for months. Daley and Goldstein described the release window as "not-so-great," acknowledging that Mario's gravitational pull at the box office left little oxygen for competing genre films.

## A Better Film Than Its Numbers Suggest

Honor Among Thieves earned roughly $208 million worldwide against a reported $150 million production budget — a modest return by blockbuster standards but far from the disaster its reputation implies. Critics largely praised the film's humor and charm, and it has since developed a genuine cult following. The directors' candor about timing suggests they believe the creative product was sound; the market conditions simply weren't.
