## Three editions, three price points

Minecraft Dungeons 2 is coming in three distinct editions, and Mojang is making sure there's a clear entry point for every type of player. The Standard Edition includes the full base game — all dungeons, all loot, all cooperative play — with no content gated behind higher tiers. If you just want to slay mobs with friends, this is the version that gets it done.

The Hero Edition adds a handful of cosmetic character skins, a pair of in-game pets, and a small bundle of bonus emeralds (the game's currency). It's the mid-tier sweet spot for players who want a bit of flair without paying full premium.

## Is the Ultimate Edition worth it?

**For collectors and completionists, the answer is almost certainly yes.** The Ultimate Edition bundles everything from the Hero Edition plus an expansion pass that guarantees access to all post-launch DLC chapters as they release. It also includes an exclusive armor set and a themed mount — cosmetic-only, but carefully designed to stand out in multiplayer lobbies.

The calculus is straightforward: if you're the kind of player who finishes a game and immediately wants more, paying extra upfront for guaranteed DLC access eliminates the à la carte headache later. For everyone else, the Standard Edition gives you the complete core experience at the lowest price.
