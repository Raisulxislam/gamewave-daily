## The Weirdest Superhero Comic of 2026 Has Arrived

If you're tired of caped heroes who play by the rules, Oni Press is about to hand you something completely different. *A Ghost Arm Made of Angry Ghosts* is the kind of superhero comic that looks like it fell out of a punk zine and landed face-first on a Ouija board — and we mean that as the highest possible compliment.

GameWave got an exclusive first look at the series, and the energy is unmistakable: chaotic, funny, and unapologetically weird. Think less spandex and more DIY tattoo parlor. The title alone tells you this isn't your standard origin story — and the interior pages deliver on that promise with a visual style that feels genuinely fresh in a market drowning in derivative superhero fare.

## Why This Matters for the Superhero Comic Genre

Superhero comics have been playing it safe for years, recycling the same formulas while indie publishers quietly innovate at the margins. *A Ghost Arm Made of Angry Ghosts* feels like a reminder that the genre's most exciting work is still happening outside the big two. Oni Press has a track record of backing bold creative voices, and this one looks like it could be a genuine cult hit.

The series doesn't have a confirmed release date yet, but based on what we've seen, it's worth keeping a close eye on your local comic shop's pull list. In a year already stacked with strong indie releases, this one stands out for sheer audacity.
