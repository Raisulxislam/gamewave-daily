## Two State of Play broadcasts on September 3

Sony has confirmed two separate State of Play presentations for September 3, giving PlayStation fans a double dose of new game reveals, updates, and announcements. The broadcasts are scheduled to air at different times throughout the day, targeting both Western and Asian audiences with distinct content windows.

## How to watch the September 2026 State of Play

Both State of Play streams will be available on PlayStation's official YouTube and Twitch channels, as well as the PlayStation website. Set your reminders for September 3 — this is shaping up to be one of Sony's most stacked State of Play lineups of the year, with rumored titles and potential platform announcements expected to fill both broadcast windows.
