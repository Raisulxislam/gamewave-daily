## A Chocolate Shop With Ghosts, Finally Moving Forward

Eric Barone, the solo developer behind Stardew Valley, has offered a rare and encouraging update on Haunted Chocolatier — his long-in-development follow-up about running a chocolate shop in a ghost-plagued town. In his own words, recent months have been "very productive," and he says he's now certain he will enjoy the finished game once it ships.

It's a notable shift in tone. Barone has previously been open about the non-linear, pressure-free nature of his development process, which has left Haunted Chocolatier without a release window for years. The fact that he's publicly expressing eagerness and drive to finish it suggests the project has reached a meaningful inflection point — though he's still not offering dates.

## Stardew's Shadow and Self-Belief

Haunted Chocolatier will inevitably live in Stardew Valley's enormous shadow, but Barone's track record makes dismissing this project foolish. Stardew Valley started as one person's passion project and became one of the best-selling indie games of all time. If the man says he's excited about what he's building, it's worth paying attention.
