Faithful steed Torrent has always been more than a mount in Elden Ring — and in the Tarnished Edition, the spectral horse gets a chance to look the part. New Torrent skins are up for grabs, and naturally, they aren't just sitting around waiting to be picked up.

## Where the Skins Live

The Torrent skins are the kind of reward that rewards commitment. Expect to track them down through tougher content, optional side paths, and encounters that the base game's map won't spell out for you. Think of each skin as a short pilgrimage rather than a quick grab.

## Unlocking Your New Look

Because the locations stay deliberately understated, the surest approach is the usual Elden Ring one: go where the challenge is. If you've been putting off that optional boss or that side dungeon you flagged for later, the Tarnished Edition gives you a fresh reason to finally head back. When you find the skin, Torrent's new look will be yours to show off across the Lands Between.
