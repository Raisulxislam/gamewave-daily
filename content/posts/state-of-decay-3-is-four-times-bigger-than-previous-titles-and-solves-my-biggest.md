State of Decay 3 is aiming big — four times bigger, in fact, than anything the series has done before.

## A World Four Times the Size

Microsoft has confirmed the undead survival sequel will open up a map roughly four times larger than previous State of Decay titles. That scale comes with gameplay shifts that directly attack the genre's oldest frustrations, especially when it comes to where you sleep and how you live.

## Multi-Base Building and Mad Max Vehicles

Headline features include building and managing multiple bases instead of clinging to one home base, a real answer to the grind that plagues many survival games. Vehicle customization follows the same spirit — a Mad Max-style approach to bolting together scrap-metal rides.

It's a direction that trades cramped repetition for freedom of movement. And with a Game Pass launch already on the books for 2027, the wait is at least attached to a concrete date.
