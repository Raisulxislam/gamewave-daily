## A Grudge Older Than the Old Republic

SWG Legends, one of the most prominent fan-run Star Wars Galaxies servers, was allegedly breached in a hack that its operators say was carried out with the help of AI tools — and carried out by someone they once trusted. According to the Legends team, a former Reddit moderator leveraged AI-assisted methods to infiltrate the server, apparently motivated by a grudge that stretched back years.

## Community Preservation Under Fire

"We're obviously bothered by this, but there isn't much we can do outside of being up front with all of you and staying focused on what makes Legends great," the team told its community. The incident throws a harsh spotlight on the fragile ecosystem of fan-run MMO preservation — projects kept alive by volunteers, often running on goodwill and donations, and perpetually vulnerable to exactly this kind of internal betrayal. Star Wars Galaxies, originally developed by Sony Online Entertainment, was shut down in 2011, and servers like Legends represent one of the only ways to still play the game today.
