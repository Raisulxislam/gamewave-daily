## A Premium Pick at a Less Painful Price

The ASUS ROG Strix G16 has long been one of our favorite high-end gaming laptops — stellar hardware, strong thermals, and a price tag that usually made you flinch. This Labor Day, Best Buy has knocked 24% off the sticker, dragging a $2,000-plus machine into significantly more comfortable territory for anyone who has been eyeing an upgrade.

## What You're Getting for the Money

Under the hood, the Strix G16 packs the kind of silicon that actually justifies its 'gaming laptop' badge. We're talking a top-tier processor paired with a capable discrete GPU, the sort of combination that lets you push modern titles at respectable settings without the laptop turning into a space heater. The discount is only available through Best Buy's week-long Labor Day event, which means the clock is ticking.

**Our take: at full price the Strix G16 is a solid buy for serious PC gamers. At 24% off, it becomes one of the smarter laptop purchases you can make right now.** Memory-module pricing volatility has driven gaming laptop costs up across the board this year, so a cut of this size on a genuinely premium machine is worth paying attention to.
