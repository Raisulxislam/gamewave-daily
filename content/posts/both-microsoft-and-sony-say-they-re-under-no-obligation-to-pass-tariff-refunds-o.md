## Who Keeps the Tariff Savings?

When import tariffs shift, someone pocket the difference — and it might not be you. Both **Microsoft and Sony** have stated plainly that they are under **no legal obligation** to pass tariff refunds through to customers who paid elevated console prices.

The stance lands awkwardly for gamers who absorbed higher price tags during periods of elevated duties. If refunds or adjustments flow back to the manufacturers, there's no mechanism forcing those savings onto receipts.

Neither company has committed to a consumer-facing rebate program, and the statements read more like legal hedge than customer goodwill. For now, anyone holding out for a tariff-adjusted refund from either camp should temper expectations.
