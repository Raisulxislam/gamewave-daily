## A Zombie Apocalypse Has Never Felt So Personal

There's something deeply surreal about fighting off undead hordes in streets you've actually walked down. God Save Birmingham, showcased at Gamescom 2026, drops players into a medieval-era zombie apocalypse set in — of all places — Birmingham, England. It's the kind of设定 that sounds absurd on paper but absolutely works in practice.

## The Bullring Has Never Been This Scary

The game leans hard into its setting. Familiar landmarks are twisted into nightmare fuel: the Bullring becomes a claustrophobic death trap, and the city's canals transform into corridors of dread. Combat is melee-focused and deliberately weighty — every swing of your weapon feels earned, and resource scarcity keeps you on edge. It's survival horror with a distinctly British sense of place, and it nails the atmosphere better than most big-budget zombie games manage.

If you've ever wanted to relive your worst Saturday shopping trip through the lens of medieval warfare, God Save Birmingham is shaping up to be exactly that experience.
