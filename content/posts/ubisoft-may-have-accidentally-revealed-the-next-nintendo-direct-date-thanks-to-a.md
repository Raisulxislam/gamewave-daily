## A Just Dance Leak Points to a Nintendo Direct This Month

Nintendo Direct dates are some of the most closely guarded secrets in the games industry — which makes it all the more ironic that a Ubisoft marketing asset may have just blown the whole thing open. eagle-eyed fans spotted what appears to be a timed Just Dance promotional tile referencing an event window that lines up neatly with a rumored September Nintendo Direct.

The asset, which was briefly live on a Ubisoft storefront before being pulled, reportedly carried metadata pointing to a specific date range. If accurate, it would suggest Nintendo is planning its next showcase in the first half of September — a window the company has historically used for fall-season Directs.

## Why Ubisoft Would Know the Date

Ubisoft has been a recurring Nintendo Direct partner for years, regularly debting Just Dance and Assassin's Creed footage during these streams. It would be entirely logical for Ubisoft to have advance notice of the date while preparing marketing materials. That said, nothing is confirmed until Nintendo says so, and the company has never been shy about changing plans at the last minute.

For now, this is one of the stronger indirect indicators we have seen for a September Direct. Nintendo typically announces its showcase only a few days in advance, so if the timeline holds, an official announcement could come at any moment.
