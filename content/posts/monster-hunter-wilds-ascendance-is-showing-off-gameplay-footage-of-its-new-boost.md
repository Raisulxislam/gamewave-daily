Capcom is letting players see the Boost Bracer in action. Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance has begun rolling out gameplay trailers for the mechanic over the next two weeks, and the first — focusing on the Great Sword — is already making waves.

## The Boost Bracer Changes Everything

The Boost Bracer is a new equipable system that fundamentally alters how each weapon plays. In the Great Sword trailer, the familiar slow, punishing charge gameplay gets layered with a burst-speed dash and a mid-combat stance shift that feels pulled straight from Devil May Cry. Players can chain heavy strikes into rapid repositioning moves, creating a rhythm that feels faster and more aggressive than any previous Monster Hunter entry.

## Devil May Cry Meets Monster Hunting

The comparisons to Devil May Cry are not subtle — and Capcom is clearly not shying away from them. The Great Sword's new moveset includes aerial transitions and stylish finishers that would not look out of place in a Dante boss fight. For a franchise historically defined by deliberate, weighty combat, the Boost Bracer represents a bold pivot toward accessibility and spectacle.

Over the next two weeks, Capcom will showcase the Boost Bracer on every remaining weapon type. If the Great Sword trailer is anything to go by, Ascendance is positioning itself as the most action-forward Monster Hunter yet — a prospect that should excite fans and terrify every Rathalos in the ecosystem.
