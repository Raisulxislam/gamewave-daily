One of the most soothing pieces of GTA 6 news in a long time: Rockstar's head of development Rob Nelson has confirmed the game will launch without microtransactions, and that no generative AI was used in its creation. The remarks came amid a wave of media visits to Rockstar North in Scotland, and echo Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick's earlier declaration that generative AI has "zero part" in what the studio is building.

## No GTA 6 Microtransactions at Launch

Rockstar never formally promised currency-free play — but given how heavily GTA Online came to lean on Shark Cards, the explicit assurance has real weight. Nelson's comments sit alongside Zelnick's portrait of worlds "built from the ground up, building by building, street by street," not stamped out by a prompt or generated procedurally.

## Handcrafted Extravagance, and What Remains Unsaid

The timing is no accident: this lands on the heels of the roughly 27-minute Extended Look that aired on Netflix, showing off Vice City, leads Jason and Lucia, and a staggeringly large map. GTA 6 arrives on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S on November 19. The honest caveat: a launch free of microtransactions isn't the same as a promise that the online side will never chase them later — that's a question for another day.
