## The Circumstantial Case Gets Stronger

A few days ago, we noted that Blizzard's StarCraft website had started glitching out in suspicious ways. At the time, it felt like a stretch. Now, the pile of coincidences has grown large enough that ignoring it takes more effort than believing it.

The StarCraft 2 website is exhibiting the same flickering, corruption-style effects. Initial skepticism about reports of a Nexon-developed StarCraft shooter — a collaboration that would have seemed unthinkable a decade ago — has softened as more corroborating details surface. And then there's the BlizzCon 2026 schedule itself, which features at least one conspicuous time block with no announced panel or event attached.

## What We Think We Know

**Here's what the landscape looks like right now:** Blizzard's StarCraft properties are displaying coordinated glitch behavior across multiple pages. A credible report links Nexon to a StarCraft-adjacent shooter project. BlizzCon's schedule has a gap that's hard to explain unless something major is being held back. None of this is confirmation. All of it is suggestive. At BlizzCon, we'll find out whether the glitch was the message — or just noise.
