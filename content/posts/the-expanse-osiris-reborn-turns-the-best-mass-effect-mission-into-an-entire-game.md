If you've spent years wishing BioWare would make another Mass Effect, **The Expanse: Osiris Reborn** might be the closest thing you'll get — and it's not even pretending otherwise. Owlcat Games, the studio behind *Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous* and *Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader*, has built a third-person sci-fi action RPG that looks, sounds, and feels like the best Mass Effect 2 mission stretched across an entire game.

## From cRPG Specialists to Space Opera

The shift is striking. Owlcat built its reputation on isometric, text-heavy CRPGs, but Osiris Reborn trades that perspective for over-the-shoulder squad combat, loyalty-driven companion arcs, and a protagonist who can talk or shoot their way through a solar-system conspiracy. You pick one of three origins — Earther, Martian, or Belter — commandeer what the studio calls "the most advanced ship in the solar system," and lead a crew whose individual storylines branch based on your choices. IGN's hands-on at gamescom 2026 came away convinced it's shaping up to be the real deal.

## Spring 2027 Can't Come Fast Enough

Owlcat has confirmed a **Spring 2027** launch across Steam, Epic, GOG, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S, with a closed beta already underway for Miller's Pack and Collector's Edition backers. Some cast members from Amazon's *The Expanse* TV series are reprising roles. The studio hasn't set a firm date yet, but if the gamescom demos are any indication, this could be the game that finally scratches that Mass Effect-shaped itch.
