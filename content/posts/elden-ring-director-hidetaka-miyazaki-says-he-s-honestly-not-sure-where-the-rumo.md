Hidetaka Miyazaki, the visionary director behind Elden Ring, has finally broken his silence on a bizarre internet rumor that has circulated for years: the claim that he has a foot fetish. Speaking in a recent interview, Miyazaki expressed genuine confusion and amusement at the speculation, calling it 'completely baseless.'

## Miyazaki's Reaction to the Foot Fetish Rumor

The rumor first gained traction on social media forums, where fans jokingly linked Miyazaki's inclusion of intricate foot armor designs in his games to a personal obsession. However, the director clarified that such design choices are purely artistic, meant to enhance character diversity and visual storytelling.

Miyazaki is known for crafting challenging, atmospheric games like Dark Souls, Bloodborne, and Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice. His creative vision has earned him a reputation as one of the industry's most innovative directors, and he remains focused on upcoming projects rather than internet memes.

## Focusing on What Matters: Game Development

Despite the lighthearted nature of the rumor, Miyazaki's response underscores the often unpredictable ways in which fan communities engage with creators. As he continues to lead FromSoftware's next ambitious title, the director's primary concern remains delivering compelling gameplay experiences.
