Grand Theft Auto's signature carjacking mechanic is getting a major overhaul in GTA 6, with Rockstar introducing new animations and contextual actions that change how players steal vehicles. For nearly three decades, the core process has remained largely unchanged—walk up, press a button, throw the driver out, and drive away.

## Stealing Cars in GTA 6 Gets a Fresh Twist

According to recent reports, GTA 6 will feature context-sensitive theft sequences that vary depending on the vehicle type, location, and even the time of day. Players might need to pick locks on older cars, use technology to bypass modern security systems, or wait for an opportune moment to strike.

## New Animations Add Depth and Realism

The overhauled animations aim to make each theft feel unique and grounded in the game's world. From hotwiring a vintage muscle car to hacking a luxury SUV's digital ignition, these details promise to elevate the carjacking experience beyond simple button presses.

Carjacking has been a core mechanic since the original Grand Theft Auto in 1997, and its evolution reflects Rockstar's commitment to innovation. While the series has always celebrated criminal escapism, GTA 6 appears to be adding layers of strategy and immersion to one of its most iconic features.
