LG's **83-inch Evo M3 4K OLED TV** — one of the largest and most feature-packed OLED panels you can buy right now — is currently available with a straight $3,000 discount, along with a five-year warranty that sweetens the deal considerably.

## What Makes the M3 Special

The Evo M3 isn't just big. LG's wireless connectivity system means you can place the TV up to 10 metres from the Zero Connect Box, running video and audio without a single HDMI cable running to the screen itself. For anyone who's ever fought cable management behind an 83-inch panel, that's a genuinely transformative feature. The OLED evo panel delivers the deep blacks and vivid colour accuracy LG's premium range is known for.

## The Value Equation at This Size

At 83 inches, the M3 crosses the line from "big TV" into "home cinema centrepiece" territory. The five-year warranty is a meaningful addition at this price point — OLED burn-in concerns, while largely solved in recent generations, are still on some buyers' minds. With the $3,000 reduction, this becomes one of the more competitive large-format OLED options currently on the market.
