Gamescom has apologized after developer hardware thefts at this year's expo in Cologne exposed an official response that many exhibitors read as callous. Notably, the apology concedes that the backlash was fair.

In a statement, organizers insist they are "committed to jointly reviewing and further improving security." It's the kind of sentence that sounds reassuring until you realize what it doesn't say.

## Gamescom's apology is a step, not a fix

Nothing in the statement addresses compensation for the affected developers — no word on replacement hardware, no financial acknowledgement. That omission is precisely what makes the apology feel hollow.

## Security pledges don't replace stolen gear

Hardware theft hits indie developers hardest: a stolen devkit or laptop isn't an insurance claim for them, it's a project setback. Gamescom can't undo the theft, but a promise about security without a remedy for the victims will always ring half-hearted.
