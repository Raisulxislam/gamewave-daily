Charlie "MoistCr1TiKaL" White has spent years building one of YouTube's most recognizable faces through deadpan commentary and cultural takes. Now he's taking his creative ambitions to the big screen — literally. A full-length animated movie adaptation of Godslap, the comic book series White created, is officially heading to theaters in 2028.

The project represents a significant step beyond the typical creator-to-screen pipeline. **White is not just lending his name to the adaptation — he's driving it as a producer**, a distinction that matters when it comes to maintaining creative control over source material that originated entirely from his imagination.

It's the latest in a wave of YouTube creators crossing over into traditional entertainment, but Godslap stands out because it didn't start as a viral moment or a brand deal — it started as a comic. For White's massive audience, the 2028 release will be a chance to see whether the creator who made millions laugh can also make them cry, or at the very least buy a ticket.
