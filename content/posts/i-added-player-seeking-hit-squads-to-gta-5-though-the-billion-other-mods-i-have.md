## Hit Squads on Your Tail

A new GTA 5 mod promises to make Los Santos feel genuinely hostile again. The mod introduces relentless, player-seeking hit squads — AI-driven teams specifically programmed to track your location and close in for the kill. It's a simple addition on paper, but in practice it transforms the rhythm of free roam into something closer to a survival game, where every corner turned could bring an unexpected firefight.

The idea taps into something GTA players have quietly wanted for years: a reason to stay on edge outside of missions. Free roam in GTA 5, after years of mods and familiarity, has become comfortable to the point of being predictable. Hit squads break that complacency by injecting genuine uncertainty into the sandbox.

## The Billion-Mod Problem

Of course, actually running the mod cleanly is another matter entirely. The creator was candid about the fact that the approximately billion other mods installed on their test setup kept interfering with the hit squads' AI behavior, creating a chaotic testing environment where the hunters sometimes couldn't find their prey. It's a familiar problem in the GTA 5 modding scene, where ambition and stability rarely coexist peacefully — and it's a reminder that the game's modding ecosystem, despite its extraordinary creativity, remains fundamentally fragile.
