## September 18, 2026 — A Fresh Start for the Franchise

The eighth *Resident Evil* film is a clean break. Director Zach Cregger, who broke out with *Barbarian* and this year's *Weapons*, is steering the franchise away from Paul W.S. Anderson's six-film saga and the poorly received 2021 *Welcome to Raccoon City*. His version is an original story set within the game universe — expect familiar vibes, but no returning heroes.

*Resident Evil* is set to premiere at Fantastic Fest on September 17 before hitting wide theatrical release on September 18. Sony Pictures is distributing, with Constantin Film and Davis Films producing. The film was shot in IMAX format in Prague during late 2025.

## A Medical Courier, Not a Super Soldier

Austin Abrams (*Euphoria*, *The Staircase*) leads as Bryan, a medical courier who delivers a package to a remote hospital in Raccoon City — only to be trapped in the snowy mountains amid a viral outbreak. The cast also includes Zach Cherry (*Severance*) as a scientist, Kali Reis (*True Detective: Night Country*) as an ex-military character, and Paul Walter Hauser (*Black Bird*) as Carl. Johnno Wilson rounds out the ensemble as Max, an Umbrella enforcer.

The decision to introduce entirely new characters rather than lean on Leon S. Kennedy or Jill Valentine signals confidence in the concept over the nostalgia. Whether that gamble pays off with audiences remains to be seen, but it's a braver creative choice than the franchise has attempted in years.
