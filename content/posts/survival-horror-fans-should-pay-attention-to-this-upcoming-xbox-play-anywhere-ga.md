A little-known ID@Xbox game just stole a moment at Gamescom 2026, and survival horror fans should be paying attention. It's called Holstin, and it arrives with Xbox Play Anywhere support — meaning one purchase carries your save and your progress across console and PC.

What makes Holstin worth flagging isn't just the pixel-art dread or the promise of Xbox Play Anywhere. It's the way it reframes the genre from the inside.

## A Perspective on Horror We Haven't Seen

Most survival horror locks you behind a fixed, cinematic camera or drops you into an over-the-shoulder chase. Holstin instead toys with perspective itself, turning the viewpoint into a puzzle and a threat. The horror isn't just in what lurks in the corridor — it's in how you're being shown it.

For players who grew up on grainy, low-res nightmares and miss when every pixel hid something, Holstin reads as a smart, genuinely fresh swing at a crowded genre. It's a title worth wishlisting before word gets out further.
