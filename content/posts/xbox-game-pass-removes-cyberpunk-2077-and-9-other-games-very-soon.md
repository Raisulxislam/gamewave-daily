## Cyberpunk 2077 Is the Latest Big Removal From Game Pass

Xbox Game Pass giveth, and Xbox Game Pass taketh away. Microsoft has confirmed the next wave of removals from the service, and the headline departure is Cyberpunk 2077, which will be pulled on September 15. It follows the removal of The Witcher 3 from the service in August — meaning CD Projekt Red has now lost its two biggest titles from Game Pass in back-to-back months.

Nine additional titles are also leaving alongside Cyberpunk 2077, though none carry quite the same weight. Still, for subscribers who haven't yet finished Night City, the clock is now ticking.

## A Pattern of High-Profile Departures

The back-to-back exits of both Cyberpunk 2077 and The Witcher 3 raise an uncomfortable question for Game Pass subscribers: how long can any major third-party title realistically stay on the service? CD Projekt Red's games were considered marquee additions when they arrived, and their relatively quick removal suggests that even blockbuster licensing deals have a short shelf life.

Microsoft continues to add games at a steady clip, but each high-profile departure chips away at the perception that Game Pass is an ever-growing library. For now, the balance between additions and removals remains the subscription's biggest ongoing debate.
