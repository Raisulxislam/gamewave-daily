## Reworking your build

If you've been wondering how to respec in **Star Wars: Zero Company**, you're not alone — choosing the wrong class or ability setup is a classic way to hit a wall mid-campaign. The good news is that the game gives you a path to undo those choices and redistribute your points.

## How to change your class and abilities

The process for resetting your class and reallocating your skills typically lives in the game's menu and hub areas rather than out in active missions. As with most action RPGs, expect to find a dedicated respec option that returns spent points to you so you can build toward a different playstyle — handy if you want to try a more aggressive or more defensive setup without starting over.

Before you spend, though, it's worth checking whether there are any costs or limits attached to respeccing, since some games gate the option behind currency or a cooldown. The smart approach is to look at what your current build is missing and respec with a clear direction in mind.
