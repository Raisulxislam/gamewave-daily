## No Mini-Maps, No Quest Markers, No Apologies

No Law arrives with a philosophy that's increasingly rare in open-world games: respect the player's intelligence. There's no UI hand-holding, no glowing quest markers pointing you toward the next objective, no tutorial that treats you like you've never held a controller. The game trusts you to explore, experiment, and figure things out.

## Every NPC Can Die — Yes, Every Single One

The sandbox commitment goes deeper than just removing waypoints. Every single NPC in the game world can be killed. That's not a gimmick — it's a design statement. Quest givers, shopkeepers, allies, enemies: nobody is safe. The narrative adapts around your choices in real time, creating a story that's genuinely yours rather than a branching path that funnels you toward a predetermined ending.

In a market saturated with open-world games that hold your hand for forty hours before letting go, No Law is a refreshing counterargument. It's the cyberpunk sandbox for players who actually want to explore a city, not just fast-travel through one.
