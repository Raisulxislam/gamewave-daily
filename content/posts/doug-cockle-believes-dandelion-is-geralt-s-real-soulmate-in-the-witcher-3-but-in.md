## Geralt and Dandelion: A Witcher 3 Soulmate Theory

Doug Cockle, the English voice behind Geralt of Rivia, has shared a hot take that's sure to divide *Witcher* fans: he believes Dandelion — known as Jaskier in the Netflix series — is Geralt's true soulmate. Before the romance crowd lights up their pitchforks, Cockle was quick to clarify the bond he's describing is entirely platonic.

Speaking about his experience voicing Geralt across *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* and its expansions, Cockle pointed to the sheer depth and longevity of Geralt and Dandelion's friendship as evidence. While players obsess over whether Geralt belongs with Yennefer or Triss, Cockle argues the bard who has been by Geralt's side through every catastrophe is the relationship that defines him most.

## Why Fans Can't Stop Debating This

The *Witcher* fandom has spent over a decade locked in the Yennefer-versus-Triss debate, so Cockle's Dandelion pick is guaranteed to ruffle feathers. But there's a genuine reading of the source material that supports it: Dandelion is the one constant in Geralt's chaotic life, the friend who shows up uninvited, overstays his welcome, and somehow becomes indispensable.

It's the kind of insight that only someone who has inhabited a character for hundreds of hours could offer. Whether you agree with Cockle or not, it's a reminder that the best friendships in gaming are often more compelling than any romance subplot — and that's worth raising a glass of Zerrikanian brandy to.
