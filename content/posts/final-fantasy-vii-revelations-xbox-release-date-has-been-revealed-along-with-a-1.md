Square Enix has officially revealed the Final Fantasy VII: Revelations Xbox release date, marking the action-RPG's highly anticipated debut on Microsoft's platform. The announcement came during Sony's September 2026 State of Play, accompanied by a 10-minute gameplay deep dive that showcased the game's combat, open-world exploration, and more.

## A Deep Dive into Revelations' Gameplay

The extended gameplay segment highlighted the title's dynamic battle system, blending real-time action with strategic command inputs. Players also got a first look at the expansive open world, featuring new regions, side quests, and environmental puzzles that promise to expand the Final Fantasy VII universe.

## Special Editions and Pre-Order Details

Alongside the release date, Square Enix announced special editions of Final Fantasy VII: Revelations, including a Collector's Edition with exclusive merchandise and a Digital Deluxe version offering bonus content. Pre-orders are now live across all platforms, with Xbox players finally able to join the adventure.

The game is the third installment in the Final Fantasy VII remake trilogy, following the critically acclaimed Final Fantasy VII Remake (2020) and Final Fantasy VII Rebirth (2024). Its Xbox release signifies a major expansion of the franchise's reach, bringing Cloud Strife's journey to a broader audience.
