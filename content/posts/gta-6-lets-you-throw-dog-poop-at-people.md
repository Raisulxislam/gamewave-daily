In GTA 6, crime truly knows no bounds — down to the most disgusting of projectile options. A newly surfaced detail confirms that players can pick up dog poop and hurl it at people in Rockstar's upcoming open-world crime game. Yes, really.

## An absurd level of simulation

Rockstar has always buried its chaos in tiny, silly interactions, and this is the latest proof. The Criminal Profile system may push you to play like a professional, but the game is equally happy to let you descend into total pettiness — and let the world witness it.

It's a small, genuinely funny slice of the sandbox that speaks to how far Grand Theft Auto 6 is going with its lived-in, reactive simulation. Whether you treat it as a weapon or just a comedy prop is entirely your call. The game hits PS5 and Xbox Series X on November 19.
