## AdHoc Studio Steps Into a Major Franchise

Supermassive Games didn't build Until Dawn 2 alone. AdHoc Studio — the relatively new outfit known for crafting the branching-narrative title *Dispatch* — has been confirmed as a co-developer on the highly anticipated horror sequel. The revelation gives us a clearer picture of how Sony assembled its team to follow up one of the most successful interactive horror experiences of the PS4 era.

## What AdHoc Brings to the Table

AdHoc Studio's DNA is rooted in narrative-heavy, choice-driven gameplay — exactly the skill set that defines the Until Dawn franchise. The studio's work on *Dispatch* demonstrated a sharp instinct for writing, pacing, and player agency across multiple story threads. Pairing that with Supermassive's technical polish and cinematic horror expertise suggests Until Dawn 2 could be one of the most narratively ambitious horror games in recent memory. For players who loved the original's butterfly-effect tension, having AdHoc in the room can only be a good sign.
