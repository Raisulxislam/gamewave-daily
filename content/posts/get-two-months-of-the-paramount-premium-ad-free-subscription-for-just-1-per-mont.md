If you've been holding out on that Paramount+ Premium subscription, this is your green light. Right now, new and returning subscribers can lock in two months of the streamer's top-tier, ad-free plan for just $1 per month — a genuinely rare discount for the no-commercials tier, which normally runs around $13.99 a month.

## How to grab the deal

Head to the Paramount+ site, pick the Premium monthly plan, and apply the promo code at checkout. The code shaves the first two months down to a dollar each. Most new and returning (non-current) subscribers should be eligible.

## The fine print

After the two promo months wrap, the plan auto-renews at the standard $13.99 monthly rate — so set a calendar reminder or cancel before then if you just want to test the waters. Two months of ad-free streams for a couple of bucks is a hard pass to ignore.
