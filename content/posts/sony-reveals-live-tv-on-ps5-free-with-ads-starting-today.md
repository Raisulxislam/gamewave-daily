## PS5 Becomes a TV Set

Sony is turning the PlayStation 5 into a television. Starting August 31, the company is rolling out Live TV on PS5 in the United States — a free, ad-supported streaming service that brings linear television channels directly to the console's dashboard. No PlayStation Plus subscription required; you just need the hardware and a tolerance for commercials.

The service aggregates a mix of live channels spanning news, entertainment, sports highlights, and lifestyle programming. Think of it less as a cable replacement and more as a discovery layer: Sony is betting that a meaningful number of PS5 owners will stumble onto live programming while navigating the console's media tab, boosting engagement metrics even when nobody is gaming.

## The Bigger Play

This move fits squarely into Sony's broader push to make the PS5 a living-room hub rather than just a gaming box. The console already supports Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+, and most major streaming apps; adding live television — even free, ad-supported live television — fills a gap that keeps eyeballs on the hardware during non-gaming hours. It also opens a new advertising revenue stream for Sony at a time when console hardware margins remain razor-thin.
