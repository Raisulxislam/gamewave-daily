For Turkish Grand Theft Auto fans, the hunger for native language support runs deep — deep enough that they've taken the localizing into their own hands.

## A Fan-Made Dub Goes Professional

A group of Turkish fans recently went beyond memes and wishes: they produced a full, professional-style Turkish dub of GTA 6's gameplay reveal. What could have been a playful fan project is polished enough to look like an official localization, voices and all.

## A Loud Statement on Localization

It's an impressive bit of community craft, but it also reads as a pointed message. Rockstar has long been selective about which languages get full treatment, and fans in markets left off the list are starting to make their argument with their own production values.

Whether it nudges the publisher is another question. But as a demonstration of passion — and patience wearing thin — the dub speaks volumes.
