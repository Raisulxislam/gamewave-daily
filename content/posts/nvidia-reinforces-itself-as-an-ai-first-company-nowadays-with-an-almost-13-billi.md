NVIDIA just spent nearly **$13 billion** to buy Hugging Face, and if anyone still thought of the company primarily as a GPU maker for gamers, that illusion is officially dead. The acquisition is the largest in NVIDIA's history and one of the biggest AI deals ever — and it tells you exactly where Jensen Huang wants this company to be in five years.

Hugging Face is the de facto open-source hub for machine learning models, datasets, and collaboration tools. It is where researchers share transformers, where startups prototype AI applications, and where a huge chunk of the global AI community already lives. By swallowing it whole, NVIDIA isn't just buying a platform — it's buying the gravitational center of open-source AI development.

This is a company that once made its name selling graphics cards to people who wanted to play *Crysis* at max settings. Now it's spending more on an AI repository than most countries spend on their entire tech budgets. The gaming division isn't going anywhere, but the corporate identity has shifted permanently. NVIDIA is an AI infrastructure company that happens to also make great GPUs, not the other way around.

The deal still requires regulatory approval, but given NVIDIA's current political capital and the bipartisan enthusiasm for AI investment in Washington, a lengthy holdup seems unlikely.
