## A New Life for Old Discs

Physical Xbox game discs are getting a second act. Microsoft has announced that owning a disc will now automatically unlock a digital licence for the same title in your Xbox library — no resale hassle, no shelf space guilt. The company is framing the move as part of a "broader commitment to game preservation," and honestly, it's hard to disagree with the sentiment.

## Why This Matters Beyond Nostalgia

For years, the ritual of swapping discs felt increasingly quaint next to the convenience of Game Pass and digital storefronts. But the shift also created a quiet anxiety among collectors: what happens to a library built on plastic? Microsoft's answer here is practical rather than sentimental — tie the disc to a permanent digital licence, and the question largely resolves itself.

The broader context is worth noting. Microsoft has spent the last decade steadily erasing friction between physical and digital ownership, from Xbox One's controversial always-online reversal to today's Smart Delivery system. This latest step feels less like a pivot and more like a logical endpoint: the disc becomes a key, not a tether.
