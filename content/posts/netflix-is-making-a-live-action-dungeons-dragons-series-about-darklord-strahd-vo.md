Netflix is going full gothic with its next big fantasy bet. The streamer is developing a live-action **Dungeons & Dragons** series set squarely in Ravenloft — the tabletop RPG's beloved horror domain — and centering it on the tragic, terrifying origin of its most iconic villain: Darklord Strahd von Zarovich.

For the uninitiated, Strahd is basically Dracula with a player character's worth of depth. He is a cursed immortal tyrant, a warlord who sold his soul for the love of a woman who didn't love him back, and he has haunted the mists of Ravenloft across decades of D&D campaigns. Making him the protagonist rather than a background menace is a bold swing — one that could finally give mainstream audiences the dark fantasy fix that *Game of Thrones* left behind.

The announcement lands at a moment when Hollywood is clearly betting hard on tabletop IP. Hasbro has been shopping D&D properties aggressively since the success of the 2023 *Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves* film, and Netflix has proven it can run long-form genre storytelling when it commits. A Ravenloft series, if done with real atmospheric commitment, could be the flagship project that proves D&D can sustain prestige television, not just popcorn movies.

No cast, showrunner, or premiere window has been confirmed yet. But the mere fact that Netflix chose Strahd over a more conventional Forgotten Realms adventure tells you exactly the kind of show they want to make: moody, character-driven, and willing to lean into horror rather than action spectacle.
