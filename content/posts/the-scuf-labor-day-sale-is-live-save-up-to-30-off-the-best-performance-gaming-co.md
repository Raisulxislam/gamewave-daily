## Premium Controllers, Discounted — Through September 13

SCUF Gaming has launched its annual Labor Day Sale, and it's one of the better opportunities to grab a tournament-grade controller without the usual $200+ sticker shock. The promotion runs through September 13 on SCUF's online store, offering up to 30% off the VALOR PRO and Envision Pro V2 lines. Prices are marked — no coupon code needed.

The standout deal is the VALOR PRO Wireless Smoke edition at $139.99, down 26% from its retail price. It features SCUF's Endurance TMR joysticks — a step above Hall Effect technology, offering finer precision and greater power efficiency through electromagnetic sensing with no moving parts. For competitive Xbox and PC players, that's legitimately premium hardware at a mid-range price.

## The Sleeper Deal: $69.99 for Hall Effect

If wireless connectivity isn't a priority, the wired VALOR PRO with Hall Effect sticks is going for $69.99 — arguably the most interesting deal in the entire sale. It eliminates stick drift at a price point that undercuts Microsoft's own Xbox Elite Series 2. The wired TMR model sits at $89.99, and the Envision Pro V2 — a PC-specific controller with five customizable G-Key hotkeys — is $125.99.

One caveat: the sale is limited to PC and Xbox controllers. SCUF's PlayStation OMEGA is not included. Discounts can't be combined with other offers and don't apply to gift cards or pending orders. But if you've been sitting on the fence about a pro controller, the $69.99 Hall Effect model is the kind of deal that makes the decision for you.
