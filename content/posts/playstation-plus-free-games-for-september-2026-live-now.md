September's **PlayStation Plus** free games are officially live, and subscribers can grab four titles starting now. The lineup covers a surprisingly wide range: tactical stealth, sports simulation, retro-flavored RPG, and chaotic party fun.

## What You Can Claim Right Now

The headliners are **Sniper Elite: Resistance** and **MLB The Show 26** — a one-two punch that covers both the stealth-action and sports camps. Rounding out the batch are **Chained Echoes**, the well-regarded indie JRPG that earned a passionate following on its original release, and **Wobbly Life**, a physics-based sandbox that plays brilliantly in co-op.

## A Strong Month for Subscribers

This is one of the more balanced PlayStation Plus months in recent memory. You get a premium franchise entry, a major annual sports title, a beloved indie gem, and a family-friendly wildcard. All four are claimable as of September 1 for any active PS Plus member.
