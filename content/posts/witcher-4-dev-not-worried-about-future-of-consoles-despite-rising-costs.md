## Rising Costs, Shrinking Optimism — Not at CD Projekt

As console hardware prices climb and trade tensions threaten to push MSRPs even higher, most of the industry is bracing for a cooling period in console-driven growth. CD Projekt joint-CEO **Michał Nowakowski**, however, is telling a different story. Speaking during a recent investor briefing, Nowakowski said he is "not worried" about the future of consoles — full stop.

## Witcher 4 Development Stays Console-First

The comment carries real weight coming from the studio behind **The Witcher 4**, one of the most anticipated RPGs on the horizon and a title being built with next-generation console hardware squarely in mind. CD Projekt has previously confirmed that The Witcher 4 — the first game in the franchise built on Unreal Engine 5 — is targeting a current-gen console and PC release, and that the project has moved fully out of its pre-production phase. Nowakowski's confidence suggests the studio sees no fundamental mismatch between rising hardware costs and the size of the addressable audience it needs.

It is a contrarian take in a moment when analysts and rival publishers have begun warning that the next console cycle could arrive later than expected, partly because manufacturing and tariff pressures are making sub-$500 hardware harder to justify. Whether that optimism proves warranted will depend on what the next twelve months of sales data actually show — but for now, CD Projekt is betting that players will still show up for the right game.
