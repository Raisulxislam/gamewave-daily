Rockstar's GTA 6 is slated to land this fall, and while most publishers are quietly scheduling around the juggernaut, Devolver Digital is charging straight into its launch window. The publisher has confirmed Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed for November 19 — and the punchline is that this self-styled GTA 6 challenger is a remaster of a remake that never existed.

## It's Not GTA 6, and That's the Point

Inventing a beloved classic and then remastering it, all with a perfectly straight face, is deeply, unmistakably Devolver. The company has built its whole brand on absurdist announcements and fake corporate theater, so 'nonexistent remake, now lovingly reslimed' is less a joke than a mission statement.

Nobody seriously expects this to dent Rockstar's release. What it does is plant a smirk in the same calendar week a worldwide smash goes to war with everything else — a reminder that game marketing is still allowed to be funny while the rest of the industry is being very, very serious about money.
