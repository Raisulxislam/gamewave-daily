## Vice City is hiding a famous landmark

Rockstar's **GTA 6** is set in Vice City — the series' Miami stand-in — and the well-known habit of populating its worlds with cultural homages seems to be alive and well. According to a fresh look at the game, Vice City will feature an apartment building that's unmistakably tied to a beloved TV show from the city's real-life skyline.

The detail is the kind of Easter egg the fan community lives for: a location that players will spot, screenshot, and share the moment they recognize it. It's also a classic Rockstar calling card, layering real-world pop culture onto its fictional sandbox.

## More than just set dressing

It's too early to say whether this landmark is purely decorative or woven into a mission, but the inclusion alone speaks to the studio's attention to detail. If the trailer and these early observations are anything to go by, Vice City is shaping up to be as loaded with recognizable touches as Los Santos ever was — and that bodes well for players who love exploring every corner.
