For the better part of two decades, Rockstar Games has operated with a near-mythical confidence — releasing once-in-a-generation titles that redefine what open-world games can be. But even Rockstar isn't immune to pressure, and studio head Rob Nelson just said the quiet part out loud.

Speaking about the upcoming GTA 6 launch, Nelson admitted the team at Rockstar North is "nervous." With 13 years of expectation piled on top of what's already the most hyped entertainment release in recent memory, that honesty is both refreshing and revealing.

## Thirteen Years of Expectation

GTA V launched in 2013. Since then, the gaming landscape has shifted dramatically — live services, battle royales, and indie blockbusters have all vied for attention. GTA 6 doesn't just need to be good; it needs to justify the wait, the cultural weight, and the staggering commercial expectations that come with being Rockstar's next move.

Nelson's admission doesn't signal doubt about the game's quality — it signals awareness of the stakes. After more than a decade, no amount of talent shields a team from the reality that this release will be judged against an almost impossible standard.
