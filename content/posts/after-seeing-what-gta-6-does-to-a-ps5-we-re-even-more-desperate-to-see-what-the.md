You only have to watch a few minutes of GTA 6 running on a PlayStation 5 to realise Rockstar has pulled off something special. The city feels alive in a way that makes the sprawling map feel less like a tech demo and more like an actual place. But once the initial wow fades, the GTA 6 PS5 footage starts raising a different question: what's left on the table for players holding out for PC?

## The Curious Gap Between Versions

Rockstar has always treated its biggest launches a little differently across platforms, and history suggests the PC release tends to arrive later — and occasionally with a bit more headroom. That gap between what the console can push and what a high-end rig could manage is exactly where the speculation starts.

## Betting on the Best Platform

Look, we're not saying the PS5 build is lacking — it absolutely isn't. But when a game already looks this good on one machine, the natural instinct is to wonder what visual tricks are being saved for the most capable hardware in the room. Until Rockstar says otherwise, that's the part we're most curious about.
