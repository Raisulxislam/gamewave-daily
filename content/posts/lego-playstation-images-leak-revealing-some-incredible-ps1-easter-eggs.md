Leaked images of a LEGO PlayStation set have surfaced, revealing a detailed recreation of Sony's iconic PS1 console. The set includes several Easter eggs that pay homage to classic gaming moments.

## A Nostalgic Build Emerges

This leak highlights the enduring appeal of retro gaming and the creative possibilities of LEGO as a medium. The photos depict a brick-built PS1 with accurate details, such as the controller ports and memory card slots.

## What the Leaks Show

Hidden inside are miniature versions of famous games and characters, delighting fans who grew up with the original console. If officially released, the LEGO PlayStation could become a collector's item bridging two beloved hobbies.
