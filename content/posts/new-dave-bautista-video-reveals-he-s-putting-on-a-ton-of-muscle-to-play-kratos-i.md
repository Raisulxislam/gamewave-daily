Dave Bautista has given God of War fans another look at the bulk-up underway for his next big role: Kratos.

In a fresh video posted to social media, the former WWE star and Guardians of the Galaxy actor shows off the body transformation he is pursuing for the God of War TV series. It is a heavyweight commitment — and the message to casting doubters is blunt.

## A Role That Demands a God's Build

Playing Kratos means carrying the frame of a mythological Spartan warrior, and Bautista is clearly treating the physical work as a full-time job. Whether the TV adaptation can match the game's brutal, cinematic scale remains the big question, but Bautista's dedication in the gym is hard to argue with.

## One Muscle at a Time

Fans have long speculated over who should embody Kratos outside the games. Bautista's willingness to transform rather than simply show up means he is shaping up as a serious contender — and the story of that transformation is becoming half the appeal.
