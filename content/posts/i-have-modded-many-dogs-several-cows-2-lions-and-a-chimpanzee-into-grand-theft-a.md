Grand Theft Auto 5's modding scene has produced some genuinely unhinged work over the years, but a post from one dedicated creator might just take the crown for sheer warmth. They've modded a whole menagerie into Los Santos — many dogs, several cows, two lions and even a chimpanzee.

The best part isn't the technical wizardry. It's the framing: every single one of these animals, by the modder's own admission, is a friend. It's the kind of gta 5 mod content that turns a chaotic sandbox into something unexpectedly wholesome.

## The animal kingdom, fully loaded

Dogs are apparently a specialty, and the creator has been modding them for a while. Add cows wandering the streets, a couple of lions asserting themselves, and a chimpanzee just vibing, and you've got a Los Santos that's suddenly a lot more interesting to slow down and observe.

## Why it's worth your time

The thread is an open invite to share your take — which means the comments are almost as entertaining as the mod itself. If you're looking for evidence that the gta 5 mod community still has plenty of heart, this is it.
