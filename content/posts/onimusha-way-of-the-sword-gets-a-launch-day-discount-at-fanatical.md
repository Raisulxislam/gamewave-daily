## Onimusha: Way of the Sword: a launch-day deal worth grabbing

This is Capcom's long-awaited revival of the supernatural samurai series that was one of the PlayStation 2 era's biggest action-adventure hits. For returning fans it's a sentimental comeback; for new players, a chance to see what all the fuss was about.

## Why grab it today

Launch-day markdowns are rare, and a first-day cut this size signals that a deeper dip isn't guaranteed. If you know you want this one, $57.39 is a fair price to pay right now. Given the loyal following the series still commands, waiting for a bigger discount isn't a bet everyone should take.
