Grand Theft Auto 6 is doubling down on the kind of realism Rockstar last explored with Red Dead Redemption 2 — and the smallest crimes now come with the biggest to-do lists. Stealing a car, by the sounds of it, will no longer be a grab-and-go affair. Theft may require planning, upkeep and one properly maintained driver.

## GTA 6's Realism Goes Deeper Than a Hotwire
Players, per early reporting, will have to keep an eye on the fuel gauge, work out to stay in shape, and eat to stay energized. A getaway car is no longer just a ride; the tank needs filling, the body needs exercise and the stomach needs attention. Each heist starts to feel like a small logistics job before the engine even turns over.

## A Chore or a Thrill?
Here is the fair criticism: realism is a great servant and a terrible master. For some players, fuelling up and staying fit will be the texture that makes the world feel alive. For others, it risks becoming homework squeezed between the fun parts. Confirmed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, GTA 6 remains the generation's most awaited release — and whether this push for realism reads as immersion or friction will decide how its most ordinary crimes are remembered.
