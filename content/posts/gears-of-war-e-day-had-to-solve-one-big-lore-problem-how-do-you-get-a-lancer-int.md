The Lancer — an assault rifle with a chainsaw bolted beneath the barrel — has been the face of Gears of War since the series debuted on Xbox 360. But Gears of War: E-Day is a prequel set right in the middle of Emergence Day, and in established canon the chainsaw Lancer wasn't created until roughly a year after the Locust poured out of the ground. So how do you put the series' defining weapon in the game without smashing the timeline?

## An Origin Lifted Straight From a Novel

The answer is a battlefield prototype with roots in the 2008 book Gears of War: The Aspho Fields, by Karen Traviss. In it, Gear soldier Tai Kaliso kills a Locust with a plain power saw in a grocery store — a moment creative director Matt Searcy says The Coalition lifted wholesale, dialogue intact, as the weapon's creation story. "Wherever possible, we've just done everything we can to respect the canon," he told GameSpot.

## The Gears of War E-Day Lancer Solve, Explained

Canon says the Lancer arrives a year after E-Day, crafted by Marcus Fenix's father; the game bridges that by introducing a prototype built during the invasion itself. Searcy acknowledges it "takes a little bit of care and attention," including the odd small retcon. The novel returns the favour too — its telling of the battle where Dom's brother Carlos dies gives Marcus and Dom's bond a richer starting point. E-Day slashes onto Xbox Series X|S and PC on October 6.
