Bandai Namco knows exactly who it's selling to. The newly revealed **Gundam Rogue Orbit Collector's Edition** is built for the Gunpla faithful — and it's leaning all the way in. The package, launching alongside the game on March 5, 2027 for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, centers on an exclusive HG 1/144 ARK-10 Gundam Helix (Earth Blue ver.) model kit that you won't find anywhere else.

## More Than Just a Pretty Box

Beyond the 15cm Gunpla, the Collector's Edition bundles the game's Launch Edition (with reversible cover art and an 11x17" poster), a premium steel case with an exclusive blueprint of the Gundam Helix, five GUNDAM Card Game promo cards, and the full Digital Deluxe content — including mission reward boosters, premium skins, and a digital artwork and music selection. It's an unabashed love letter to fans who've been building model kits long before they ever picked up a controller.

## A Bold New Gundam Chapter

Gundam Rogue Orbit itself is a departure for the franchise: a fast, high-mobility action game set in an all-new universe rather than a retread of existing timelines. Developed in part under the "RG Project" umbrella — which ties into the anime *Mobile Suit Gundam RG XARX-ZERO* — it's built as both an entry point for newcomers and an evolution for longtime fans. The Collector's Edition just makes it clear that Bandai Namco is betting on the intersection of gaming and gunpla culture in a way few publishers would dare.
