The GTA 6 reveal is a study in extremes, and I walked away both thrilled and quietly let down. Vice City looks genuinely spectacular — a neon-drenched open world begging to be roamed. And yet, behind the shine, I kept catching glimpses of a mission template I've already played a hundred times.

The reveal wears its ambition on its sleeve, and visually it's hard to fault. But for a studio that built its name on reinventing the open-world genre, the mission beats in the footage feel worryingly familiar.

## Vice City Has Never Looked Better

This is the Vice City you've been dreaming of since the PS2. Sun-bleached streets, dense nightlife, a living city that feels built to be explored on foot and at speed. As a setting, it's nearly flawless.

## Same Old Rockstar Song

Then the missions roll, and my excitement cools. Chase here, escape there, dead-eye shootout in the middle — the hooks that made GTA great are still here, but they're wearing the same coat. There's a reason "expect the same missions" cuts both ways: for better, and for worse.

The reveal promises a gorgeous world. I just wish Rockstar looked as eager to shake up how we play in it as they clearly are to show it off.
