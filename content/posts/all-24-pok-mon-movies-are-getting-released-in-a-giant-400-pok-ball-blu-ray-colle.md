## The Ultimate Pokémon Movie Collection Fits Inside a Poké Ball

Pokémon fans with deep shelves and deeper pockets have a new centerpiece to chase. A complete Blu-ray collection of all 24 Pokémon animated films is being released in a specially designed Poké Ball-shaped case, and preorders are already live at $400.

The set collects the entire theatrical Pokémon filmography — from the original 1998 film through to the most recent releases — into a single, gravity-defying package that doubles as a display piece. At that price, it's clearly positioned as a premium collector's item rather than a casual purchase, and the Poké Ball housing is designed to sit prominently on a shelf or desk.

## Why This Collection Matters Right Now

The timing is notable. Physical media has been in retreat for years, with streaming platforms eating into disc sales across the entertainment industry. A $400 box set signals that there's still a dedicated market for premium physical releases — particularly for beloved franchises with multi-generational fanbases. Pokémon, which has been producing animated films for nearly three decades, is uniquely positioned to justify a collection of this scale.

The $400 price point also places it in the company of other luxury anime and animation box sets that have found success with collectors willing to pay for presentation and completeness. Whether the content includes new bonus features or restorations beyond the existing Blu-ray releases remains to be seen, but the package itself is clearly the star here.
