Come for the open-world, stay for the open-mouth memes. Netflix's extended Grand Theft Auto 6 preview has dropped, and while Rockstar clearly packed the footage with detail for fans to pore over, the internet has seized on something far more unexpected: Jason's voice is giving off major Bane energy.

The comparison is everywhere, and honestly, it's not hard to see why. Between the delivery and the sheer intensity Jason brings to the screen, players have decided the lead is channeling a certain masked villain from another franchise. Fair? Debatable. Entertaining? Absolutely.

## Why the Bane-itis comparison is sticking

It's the kind of thing no amount of leak analysis can prepare you for. Rockstar can polish ray-traced reflections and pack the frame with physics to their heart's content, but the moment a fanbase glues onto a vocal quirk, the discourse belongs to the memes.

## The footnotes we keep coming back to

What makes it fun is that nobody can agree whether it's a compliment. Half the thread is arguing Jason sounds intimidating, the other half is convinced it's unintentional. Either way, the GTA 6 preview has already done exactly what a teaser is supposed to do — it got people talking, even if not about what Rockstar intended.
