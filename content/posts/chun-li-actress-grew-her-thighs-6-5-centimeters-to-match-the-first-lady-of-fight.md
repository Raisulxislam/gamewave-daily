Callina Liang, who plays Chun-Li in Legendary's upcoming Street Fighter movie, took the role's physical demands literally: she grew her thighs 6.5 centimeters to match the First Lady of Fighting Games' iconic silhouette. The transformation, she says, came down to a protein-heavy diet and an intense training routine — a signal of just how seriously the film is taking the character.

## What the Street Fighter Movie Gets Right

Chun-Li's defining trait has always been her legs, treated in the games not as a punchline but as the engine of her fighting style. Liang's commitment is the kind of role-embracing dedication action cinema loves to talk about and rarely shows in the final cut — the grunt work behind the pretty frames.

It matters because video game adaptations usually sell the fantasy through stunt doubles and CGI. Seeing an actor actually sweat to inhabit a signature character is a welcome vote for old-school craft — and a reminder that muscles earned honestly carry more weight than any effects pipeline.
