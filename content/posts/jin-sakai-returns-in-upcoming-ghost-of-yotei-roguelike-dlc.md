## The Ghost Comes Back for More

During today's PlayStation State of Play livestream, Sucker Punch pulled back the curtain on Ghost of Yotei's upcoming roguelike DLC, officially titled Most Wanted. **And the headline feature is the return of Jin Sakai** — the protagonist of Ghost of Tsushima — as a playable character within the new mode.

The roguelike format is a natural fit for Ghost's combat system. Procedurally arranged encounters, escalating difficulty, and permadeath risk all play to the series' strengths: precise swordplay, tactical stealth, and that intoxicating flow state the original games cultivated so carefully.

## Why This Matters

Bringing Jin Sakai back through a roguelike framework rather than a traditional narrative expansion is a smart creative choice. It lets Sucker Punch honor the character without committing to a full story continuation, while giving players the kind of replayable, skill-driven challenge that extends a game's life well beyond its credits. **For Ghost of Tsushima fans who never got enough of that combat loop, Most Wanted could be exactly the fix.**
