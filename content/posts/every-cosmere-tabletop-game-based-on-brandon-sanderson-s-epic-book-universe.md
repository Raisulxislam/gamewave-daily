## A Universe That Keeps Growing

Brandon Sanderson's Cosmere — the interconnected literary universe spanning Mistborn, Stormlight Archive, and beyond — has been expanding steadily on the tabletop front. What started with a handful of ambitious Kickstarter projects has matured into a genuine board-game franchise with multiple titles across different play styles.

The **Mistborn: House War** board game remains one of the earliest entries, letting players compete for political control of the Final Empire through resource management and negotiation. Alongside it, the **Stormlight Archive: Call to Adventure** board game uses a narrative-driven deck-building mechanic that captures the epic scope of Sanderson's most sprawling series.

## What's Available Now and What's Coming

More recently, **Mistborn: The Board Game** launched as a cooperative adventure that faithfully translates Sanderson's Allomantic magic system into a push-your-luck dice mechanic — a design choice that fans have praised for feeling both strategic and true to the source material.

Sanderson's company Dragonsteel Entertainment has also confirmed additional Cosmere titles in various stages of development. While specific details remain under wraps, the ambition is clear: to make the tabletop space a primary home for Cosmere storytelling, not just a licensed afterthought. For fans, that means a steady pipeline of games that take the source material seriously.
