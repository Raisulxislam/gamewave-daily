## SteamDB Changes Hands — But the Essentials Stay Free

SteamDB, the invaluable community tool for tracking Steam prices, player counts, and release data, has been acquired by Nexus Mods. The transition marks a major shift for one of PC gaming's most relied-upon resources, and it immediately raises a question every user is asking: what changes?

According to the announcement, the answer is: not what you think. Nexus Mods has explicitly pledged that there will be no ads, no paywalls, and no features taken away from existing users. "Nothing you know and use today is getting taken away and sold back to you," the statement reads.

## Why SteamDB Needs Revenue — and Why That's Not Inherently Bad

SteamDB has operated for years largely as a passion project, and sustaining that kind of operation indefinitely without revenue is simply not viable. Nexus Mods stepping in provides a financial backbone, and the commitment to keeping the core experience free is meaningful — especially from a company that has built its own reputation on supporting modding communities without gating access.

The bigger test will come over time: whether Nexus Mods can maintain this promise as the pressures of monetization inevitably grow. For now, though, PC gamers can breathe a cautious sigh of relief.
