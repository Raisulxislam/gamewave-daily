## Rising RAM Prices Won't Slow CD Projekt Red Down

The gaming industry has been buzzing about the possibility that next-generation consoles could be delayed thanks to soaring memory chip prices — but CD Projekt Red joint CEO Michal Nowakowski says his studio isn't losing sleep over it.

In a recent interview, Nowakowski acknowledged the reality of rising hardware costs but made it clear that CD Projekt Red's development timeline isn't being derailed by component pricing. The studio, currently deep in development on the next *The Witcher* installment and its *Cyberpunk 2077* successor, appears to be taking the long view on the console cycle.

## Why This Matters for the Industry

RAM prices have been climbing steadily throughout 2026, driven by demand from AI data centers and consumer electronics alike. Industry analysts have flagged the possibility that Sony and Microsoft might need to push back next-gen hardware launches if component costs remain elevated — a scenario that would ripple across the entire games ecosystem.

Nowakowski's confidence suggests CD Projekt Red may be positioning its major releases to be platform-agnostic, or at least not exclusively dependent on next-gen hardware arriving on schedule. For gamers, the takeaway is straightforward: the studio's biggest titles should still land within a reasonable window, regardless of what the console market does.
