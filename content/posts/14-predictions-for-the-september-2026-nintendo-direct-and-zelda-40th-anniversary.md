Nintendo is gearing up for what could be one of its most stacked Direct windows in recent memory. With a September 2026 showcase on the horizon and a dedicated Zelda 40th Anniversary broadcast following close behind, the rumor mill is running at full speed — and IGN's Logan Plant has weighed in with a curated list of 14 predictions.

## The Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct Could Be a Milestone Moment

The Legend of Zelda turns forty in 2026, and Nintendo has rarely let an anniversary pass without a major statement. Plant's picks lean into long-rumored remasters and remakes that fans have been begging for, alongside the possibility of a brand-new entry that could redefine the franchise's next decade. Whether or not every prediction lands, the sheer number of plausible reveals speaks to how much pent-up anticipation surrounds this anniversary.

## Cross-Platform Surprises and Release Window Reveals

Plant's list isn't limited to Zelda. Several predictions point to broader Nintendo ecosystem reveals — potential collaborations, third-party announcements, and possible release window confirmations for titles that have been quietly teased over the past year. If even a handful of these materialize, this Direct cycle could set the tone for Nintendo's holiday season and well into 2027. The full predictions are worth a read for anyone who wants to get ahead of the hype curve.
