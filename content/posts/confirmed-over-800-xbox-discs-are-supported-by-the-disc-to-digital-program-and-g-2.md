## A Disc-to-Digital Library That's Already Massive

When Xbox first teased its disc-to-digital program, many assumed it would launch as a modest pilot — a handful of backwards-compatible classics, maybe a few marquee titles to make the headlines. Instead, Microsoft has quietly built out a compatibility database that already spans **over 800 discs**, and the number is climbing as community-verified lists circulate online. That is a staggering scale for a feature still rolling out to Xbox Insiders in the Alpha and Alpha Skip-Ahead rings.

## Why It Matters for Physical Game Collectors

The practical upside is obvious: anyone sitting on a stack of old Xbox 360 or original Xbox cases can now convert them to digital licences without hunting down individual store listings. For collectors, it closes a long-standing gap between ownership and convenience. **The program essentially treats your physical disc as a proof-of-ownership token**, turning a shelf of plastic into a cloud library — and it does so without requiring you to keep the disc spinning in a tray.

What remains to be seen is how aggressively Microsoft expands the catalogue beyond what was already backwards-compatible. If the current pace is any indication, disc-to-digital could quietly become one of the most consumer-friendly moves in Xbox's recent history.
