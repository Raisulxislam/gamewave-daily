## A Tighter, More Intentional RPG

Playground Games has confirmed that the **Fable reboot's main story will take approximately 15 to 20 hours to complete** — a clear signal that the studio is aiming for a tighter, more deliberate RPG experience rather than the hundred-hour open-world marathon that has become common in the genre.

That timeframe is a statement of intent. In an era when 40-plus-hour main campaigns are treated as the default, Playground is betting that players will respond to a game that respects their time. The original Fable was celebrated precisely for this quality: a compact, flavourful adventure that didn't pad itself with busywork.

## What This Tells Us About the Design

The 15-to-20-hour window suggests Playground has resisted the temptation to inflate scope simply because the technology allows it. The Fable reboot runs on Unreal Engine 5, and the team has shown off environments that are visibly more ambitious than anything the series has attempted before. But **scope and length are not the same thing**, and Playground appears to understand that distinction.

There will almost certainly be side content, collectibles, and reasons to linger in Albion beyond the credits. The question is whether the core experience feels complete on its own — and at 15 to 20 hours, the developers are clearly signalling that it should.
