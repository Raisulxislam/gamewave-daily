## Story missions restrict character swapping

Rockstar is reportedly locking character swapping out of GTA 6's story missions. During narrative-driven sequences, you'll play as whichever protagonist the story demands — no hot-swapping between Lucia and Jason at will. It's a deliberate choice to preserve pacing and emotional continuity during key story beats.

## Free roam keeps the flexibility players expect

Outside of missions, full character-swapping freedom is expected to remain intact. The restriction is narrative-specific, not a blanket limitation — Rockstar is clearly prioritizing storytelling cohesion over the sandbox chaos of character hopping. Given how GTA 5's character-switching became a defining feature, the decision to gate it during story missions suggests the narrative ambitions of GTA 6 are the real showpiece this time around.
