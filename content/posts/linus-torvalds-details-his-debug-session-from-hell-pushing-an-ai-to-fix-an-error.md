## A battle of wills with a stubbornal AI

Even Linus Torvalds isn't immune to a truly miserable debugging day. The Linux creator recently recounted what he lightly calls a "debug session from hell," in which he pushed an AI assistant to keep working on an error it repeatedly insisted was "impossible and unsolvable."

## When the machine refuses to budge

The clash is familiar to anyone who's sparred with a stubborn model: the tool assured him the problem had no fix, while Torvalds remained convinced otherwise — and refused to accept the AI's dead-end verdict. After the long back-and-forth, he made the model write up its own report on the whole ordeal.

It's a revealing snapshot of the current state of AI-assisted debugging — powerful enough to dig into gnarly problems, but still capable of talking itself into a corner. Torvalds' takeaway, as ever, is that the human in the room has to be the one who refuses to give up.
