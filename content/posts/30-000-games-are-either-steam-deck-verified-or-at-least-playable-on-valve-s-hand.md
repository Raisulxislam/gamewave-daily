Valve's Steam Deck has reached a new milestone: over 30,000 games are now either verified or playable on the handheld. This growth underscores the device's expanding appeal among PC gamers.

## A Growing Library for Portable PC Gaming

The rapid increase in compatible titles shows that developers are embracing the platform, making the Steam Deck a more viable option for on-the-go gaming. The Steam Deck launched in February 2022, and since then, the verification program has steadily grown.

## What Verification Means for Players

Steam Deck verification ensures games run smoothly on the device, with proper controls and performance. Playable titles may require some tweaks but are still functional. This distinction helps players choose experiences optimized for the handheld.

As the library expands, the Steam Deck continues to solidify its position in the handheld market.
