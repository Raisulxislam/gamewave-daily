Rockstar has finally spelled out one of Grand Theft Auto 6's most important systems, and fans of Red Dead Redemption 2 will feel right at home. Building on the classic Wanted levels from past GTA titles and the Honor meter from Red Dead 2, GTA 6 introduces a Criminal Profile system that tracks how violently players run their crime sprees.

Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson framed it as a story-first choice: "This is a story about a couple on the run." Commit a hit-and-run, move on before the cops arrive, don't escalate — and you'll generally be fine. Turn everything to blood and mayhem, and the game starts to notice. Play professionally and cleanly, and your profile stays strong.

## Not a right-and-wrong meter

The key twist: this isn't a moral compass pushing Jason and Lucia to be do-gooders. It's a gauge of how malicious and gratuitous your violence gets — unnecessary carnage drags it down, while a clean, professional heist keeps your reputation intact.

## What changes when it drops

The world reacts. Squeeze your Criminal Profile too low and Leonida's NPCs start treating the pair very differently — a reminder that in GTA 6 you're no longer the only dangerous person on the street. Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch for PS5 and Xbox Series X on November 19.
