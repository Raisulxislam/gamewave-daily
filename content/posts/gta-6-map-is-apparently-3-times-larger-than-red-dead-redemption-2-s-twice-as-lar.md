## GTA 6 map talk: the bigger picture

If either figure holds up, Rockstar is betting its identity on extreme scale. But the two claims don't triangulate cleanly with each other, which is exactly why the arithmetic should stay at rumor level for now. What's beyond dispute is that GTA 5's world was already vast — doubling it means designing everything around the journey itself.

## Enjoy the numbers, wait for the map

Until Rockstar draws the whole thing itself, every 'leaked' size is a rumor wearing a suit. Map size is easy to hype and hard to verify, and in this case the only defensible move is patience. The map, like the game, will arrive when Rockstar is good and ready.
