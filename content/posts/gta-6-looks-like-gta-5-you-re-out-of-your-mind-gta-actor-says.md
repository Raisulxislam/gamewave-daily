## "You're Out Of Your Mind"

The response to GTA 6's recent "extended look" trailer has been overwhelmingly positive, but one criticism keeps surfacing: the game looks too much like GTA 5. It's a take that has gained surprising traction on social media, and one person who has zero patience for it is Ned Luke — the actor behind Michael De Santa in GTA 5.

Luke didn't mince words. In his view, anyone comparing the two games visually is, to put it plainly, out of their mind. And honestly, the frustration is understandable. GTA 5 was released in 2013 and received a current-gen remaster, but GTA 6 represents a generational leap in rendering technology, environmental density, and animation fidelity. Criticizing it for sharing a tonal lineage with its predecessor while ignoring the astronomical technical gap between them is, at best, a shallow read.

## The Real Gap Is in the Details

The trailer showcases facial capture quality that is miles ahead of anything in GTA 5's remaster, alongside environmental systems — weather, lighting, vegetation — that operate at an entirely different level of complexity. Whether you love or hate the artistic direction, calling it a visual clone of an eleven-year-old game doesn't hold up to even casual scrutiny.
