## The Cradle of War Has Arrived — Udders and All

When Fenris Creations (formerly CCP Games) released the *Cradle of War* update for EVE Online this summer, the community immediately shortened it to "CoW." Someone at Fenris clearly noticed, because an actual cow is now livestreaming an EVE Online session from Iceland, and it is every bit as absurd and wonderful as that sentence implies. The stunt has turned into one of the most talked-about marketing moments in the MMO space this year.

## A Community梗 Turned Into Content Gold

The EVE Online player base has always thrived on chaos, community in-jokes, and the kind of absurdist humor that makes other MMO communities look positively tame. Fenris leaning into the CoW abbreviation and building a full livestream event around a real cow is a masterclass in reading your audience and giving them exactly the kind of weird joy they crave. Whether the cow actually managed to warp to a gate or not is beside the point — the meme has become the message, and EVE players are eating it up.
