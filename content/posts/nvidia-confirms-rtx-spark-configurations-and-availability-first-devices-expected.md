The wait for **NVIDIA RTX Spark** hardware is nearly over. NVIDIA has confirmed that the first devices powered by its N1X chip will begin shipping as early as October 2026, arriving in two distinct configurations that should cover a range of use cases from creative work to portable gaming.

Both configurations are built around NVIDIA's N1X processor, but they diverge on memory and GPU allocation. The details point to NVIDIA aiming squarely at the premium portable space — a segment where ARM-based efficiency and dedicated RTX graphics could make a serious dent against x86 incumbents. Having two SKUs at launch is a smart move; it gives OEM partners flexibility and gives early adopters a genuine choice rather than a one-size-fits-all compromise.

This launch matters beyond the spec sheet. RTX Spark represents NVIDIA's most tangible push yet into devices where the company controls the full silicon story, not just the discrete GPU. If the October rollout goes smoothly, it could reshape expectations for what ARM-powered Windows machines can actually deliver in real-world creative and gaming workflows.

Exact pricing and OEM partner names have not been fully detailed yet, but expect a wave of announcements as we get closer to the October ship window.
