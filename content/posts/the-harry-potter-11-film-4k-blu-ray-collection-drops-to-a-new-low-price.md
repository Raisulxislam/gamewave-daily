Physical media collectors, your shelf just got a lot more tempting. The **Harry Potter and Fantastic Beasts 11-Film 4K Blu-ray Collection** — all eight original Wizarding World films plus the three *Fantastic Beasts* prequels — has dropped to its lowest price yet on Amazon.

## Every Film, One Box, Stunning 4K

Released on May 19 to mark the 25th anniversary of *Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone*, this set packs every film into a single slipcased collection with HDR10 mastering and DTS:X audio. Amazon currently has it listed well below its original list price, making it one of the better deals available for anyone building a physical 4K library. The UK market has also seen the price slide, with Amazon.co.uk tracking around C$125.72 in Canada and comparable drops in the US.

## A Collector's Window Before the Steelbooks Arrive

Here's the thing: if you're on the fence, you may want to act before October. A brand-new 16-disc UK 4K 25th Anniversary Steelbook collection has already been announced for later that month, which could shift attention and shelf space. For now, though, this standard edition is genuinely hard to beat at its current price — and it covers every corner of the Wizarding World in one shot.
