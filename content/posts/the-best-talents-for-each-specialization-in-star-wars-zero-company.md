## How Specializations Work in Zero Company

Each specialization in Zero Company gives your character a unique skill tree and playstyle. The game rewards thoughtful talent allocation — investing deeply in one path often yields more powerful results than spreading points thin. That said, hybrid builds have their place, especially in co-op where squad synergy matters more than individual power.

The community is still theorycrafting optimal builds, so there's room to experiment. But the talent tiers are already crystallizing around clear favorites for each specialization — abilities that either provide indispensable utility or raw damage output that no other pick can match.
