Watch the Gamescom footage that surfaced this week and you would be forgiven for doing a double-take: **Exterminauts looks, at first glance, very much like Helldivers 2.** Both are cooperative PvE shooters in which squads of heavily armed players push through waves of hostile creatures. The team at Behaviour Interactive, however, insists the resemblance ends at first contact.

## Why Exterminauts Says It Stands Apart

On paper the formula is familiar — squads, oversized guns, creature hordes and bigger explosions still to come. Where Exterminauts wants to differ, the developers argue, is in its own rhythm: loadouts, enemy behaviour and mission flow built around teamwork rather than a mirror of Arrowhead's runaway hit. This is a studio that knows something about carving identity out of a crowded genre — Behaviour Interactive is the house behind Dead by Daylight — and it is betting on the same trick here.

## The Comparison Is a Gift and a Burden

It is easy to dismiss the whole thing as *Helldivers with a paint job*, and the team seems comfortable with that first impression — which is smart, because it gets players through the door. The harder task is making the stay worthwhile. If this week's footage is any guide, creature design and squad play are where Exterminauts intends to draw its own line, and that is exactly where the comparison stops being useful.
