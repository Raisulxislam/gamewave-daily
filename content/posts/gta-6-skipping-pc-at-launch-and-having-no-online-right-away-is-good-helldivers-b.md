There's a contrarian take doing the rounds, and it comes from one of the industry's most decorated studio heads. The Helldivers 2 boss has publicly backed GTA 6's choice to skip PC at launch and ship without online right away — **a staggered rollout, in his view, is a good thing.**

## Why GTA 6 Could Win With a Slower Start

Focus sits at the core of the argument: one platform, one mode, one thing to get right at a time. A game that launches without its live layer can lock down the foundation before the player flood arrives, and a console-first window keeps attention from splitting three ways.

## The Case Against Waiting

Not everyone is persuaded, and the pushback writes itself: PC owners feel left out, online-hungry players bristle, and a tiered platform plan still means tiered pain for anyone locked out of day one. Both readings are defensible, which is exactly why this is a conversation worth having.
