## Resident Evil's Popcorn Bucket Goes Full Horror

The tradition of increasingly unhinged movie popcorn buckets continues, and the upcoming Resident Evil film from Weapons director Zach Cregger has just added one of the most appropriately disturbing entries to the growing list.

The bucket, revealed for at least one theater chain, leans heavily into the franchise's signature body horror aesthetic. Details are still emerging, but early images show a design that is, depending on your perspective, either a must-have collectible or a genuinely unsettling object to carry through a crowded lobby. That tension is entirely the point.

## The Popcorn Bucket Arms Race

The movie popcorn bucket has evolved from a forgettable cardboard cylinder into a genuine cultural event. Recent blockbusters — from Dune's sandworm design to Godzilla's jaw-shaped vessel — have turned what was once a throwaway purchase into a marketing centerpiece and social media magnet. Studios have figured out that a well-designed bucket generates free publicity worth far more than the production cost.

For the Resident Evil franchise, the gross-out popcorn bucket is almost too perfect a match. The series has built its reputation on grotesque creature designs and body horror that makes audiences squirm, so a popcorn container that looks like it might bite back feels less like a gimmick and more like a natural extension of the brand. Zach Cregger, whose previous film Weapons demonstrated a flair for visceral, uncomfortable imagery, appears to be leaning into that sensibility from the marketing stage onward.
