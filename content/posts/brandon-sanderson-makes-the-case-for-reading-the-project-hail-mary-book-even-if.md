## The Book Still Has Secrets to Tell

Seeing the movie doesn't close the book on **Project Hail Mary**. Bestselling author **Brandon Sanderson** has made the case that Andy Weir's sci-fi novel remains a rewarding read even for audiences who already know how the story ends.

Sanderson's argument centers on what prose can do that film cannot: internal monologue, scientific problem-solving played out in real time, and a layer of dry humor that doesn't always survive adaptation. For readers who enjoy the mechanics of survival storytelling, the book offers a depth the screen version necessarily trims.

The Project Hail Mary film, directed by Phil Lord and Christopher Miller, brought fresh attention to Weir's novel. But Sanderson's endorsement is a useful nudge for anyone who assumed the adaptation made the source redundant.
