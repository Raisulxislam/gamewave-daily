## The List Is Bigger Than Expected

Microsoft's disc-to-digital initiative — the program that lets Xbox owners swap a physical disc for a digital license without paying full price — has crossed a quietly impressive milestone. A community-maintained database tracking supported titles has confirmed that over 800 Xbox discs are now eligible for conversion, and the list continues to grow.

## A Useful Backstop for Physical Collectors

For gamers who still buy discs but want the convenience of a digital library, the program solves a real friction point. Pop in a compatible title, pay a modest conversion fee, and you have a digital copy tied to your account — no shelf space required. The database, which has been painstakingly catalogued by community members, gives a clear picture of exactly what works and what doesn't, something Microsoft's own documentation has been slower to clarify.

**The bigger picture: 800 titles is no longer a pilot program — it's a full-fledged feature that signals Microsoft's continued willingness to bridge the gap between physical and digital ownership.** As the list grows, it becomes an increasingly compelling reason for disc-buying Xbox owners to consider making the jump.
