Milly Alcock's **Supergirl** is officially headed to HBO Max next Thursday, September 10, Warner Bros. confirmed this week. The DC Studios film — directed by Craig Gillespie and based on Tom King and Bilquis Evely's *Supergirl: Woman of Tomorrow* — will also premiere on HBO's linear channel two days later on September 12 at 8 p.m. ET.

## A Tight 76-Day Theatrical Window

The streaming arrival comes just 76 days after the film's June 26 theatrical debut, squarely in line with the roughly 77-day window Warner Bros. has stuck with for every major 2026 release except *Superman*, which landed on Max after just 70 days at James Gunn's request to help audiences catch up before *Peacemaker* Season 2.

Supergirl has been available to buy or rent digitally since July 28, and physical media fans can pick up the 4K Ultra HD, Blu-ray, and DVD editions starting September 8 — all packed with over 90 minutes of bonus featurettes. For subscribers, though, the wait is almost over: next Thursday, Kara Zor-El finally comes home to streaming.
