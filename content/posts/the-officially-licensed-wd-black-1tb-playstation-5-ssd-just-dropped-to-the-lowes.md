Good deals on PS5 storage are practically non-existent in 2026 — until now. Amazon-owned Woot is offering the **officially licensed WD Black SN850P 1TB PS5 SSD** for **$179.99** after applying a coupon code, marking the drive's lowest price of the year.

## Built for PS5, Priced for Everyone

The SN850P isn't just any NVMe drive. It's manufactured specifically for the PS5 console with an exclusive heatsink design that slots directly into the M.2 bay with zero compatibility headaches. Read speeds hit up to 7,300MB/s, and the drive meets Sony's minimum 5,500MB/s requirement with plenty of headroom to spare. It's backed by a 5-year WD warranty, which is a nice safety net for a component you're trusting with your entire library.

## Why Now Matters

With GTA 6's launch on the horizon and a packed fall release calendar — including *Supergirl*, *Lanterns*, and *Clayface* across entertainment — storage anxiety is about to peak. PS5 owners sitting on a mostly-full drive know the drill: deleting and re-downloading games because the internal SSD filled up faster than expected. At this price, the SN850P becomes an easy, no-brainer impulse buy for anyone who's been putting off the upgrade.
