## Senators Call Out Discord and Roblox on Child Safety

Three US Democratic senators have taken aim at both Discord and Roblox, arguing that the platforms should face open court proceedings rather than shielding themselves through private arbitration. In a joint statement, the lawmakers said both companies "deserve their day in court" over their handling of child safety on their respective platforms.

The criticism centers on a familiar playbook: when faced with lawsuits or regulatory scrutiny, many tech companies compel users — including minors — into private arbitration clauses that keep cases out of public courts. Senators argue this practice allows Discord and Roblox to avoid meaningful accountability while young users remain at risk.

## The Bigger Picture for Platform Accountability

Discord and Roblox both host millions of minors daily, and both have faced prior scrutiny over predatory behavior, inappropriate content exposure, and inadequate age verification. Roblox in particular has drawn repeated criticism for its younger user base, with critics arguing the platform's open-creation model makes it uniquely difficult to moderate.

This push from Capitol Hill comes as Congress continues to ramp up pressure on tech companies over child safety, a rare bipartisan issue in an otherwise divided legislature. Whether it leads to actual legislation or remains rhetorical pressure, the message is clear: the era of quietly settling child-safety lawsuits behind closed doors may be ending.
