## Half-Year, Full Wallet

CD Projekt Red closed the first half of 2026 with numbers that most independent studios would kill for. The Polish RPG powerhouse's latest earnings report confirms the company remains on a strong financial trajectory, buoyed by the long-tail sales of Cyberpunk 2077 and The Witcher 3 — titles that continue to generate revenue years after launch.

**The real story, though, is forward-looking.** With The Witcher 4 deep in development and additional unannounced projects reportedly in the pipeline, CDPR's current cash reserves position it to fund ambitious multi-year development cycles without the external pressure that sank so many other studios in recent years.

## Why It Matters

The Witcher 4 is arguably the most anticipated RPG in development right now. A healthy balance sheet means CDPR can afford to take the time it needs rather than rushing to market — a lesson apparently learned the hard way with Cyberpunk 2077's troubled 2020 launch. For players, that patience could translate into the kind of polished, uncompromised experience the studio is capable of delivering when it isn't racing the calendar.
