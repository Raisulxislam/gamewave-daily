## Finally, a Price That Doesn't Make You Wince

PC prices have been climbing steadily for years, driven by component shortages, tariffs, and the relentless push toward premium tiers. Samsung's new Galaxy Book6, priced at $799, feels like a deliberate counterpoint. It's not trying to be a MacBook killer or a gaming powerhouse — it's trying to be a reliable, well-built laptop that doesn't require a second mortgage.

## Battery Life That Actually Delivers

The standout feature isn't any single spec — it's the balance. Samsung has focused on battery life as a headline metric, and early impressions suggest it delivers genuinely all-day endurance. For students, remote workers, and anyone tired of hunting for outlets, that alone justifies the price of admission. The build quality punches above its weight class too, with a chassis that feels more expensive than the sticker price suggests.

In a market where mid-range often means "compromise in every direction," the Galaxy Book6 is a refreshing reminder that sensible, well-priced laptops still exist.
