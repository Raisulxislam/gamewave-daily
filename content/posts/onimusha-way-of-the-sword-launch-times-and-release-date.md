## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Release Date Is Set

Capcom's long-awaited Onimusha: Way of the Sword now has a confirmed release date and a full slate of global launch times. The revival of the classic samurai action series is finally giving fans a concrete date to mark on their calendars.

The original Onimusha trilogy was a PlayStation 2-era staple, and this new entry marks Capcom's first serious return to the franchise in years. With the studio riding high on the success of Resident Evil remakes and Monster Hunter Wilds, there is real momentum behind this launch.

## When Does Onimusha: Way of the Sword Launch?

Global rollout times have been confirmed across all major platforms. Players should check their regional storefronts for the exact unlock moment — Capcom has staggered the launch by time zone to ensure a smooth global debut.

For a franchise that many assumed was dormant for good, the level of marketing push and coordinated launch planning here signals that Capcom is treating Onimusha as a major tentpole release, not a niche nostalgia play.
