If you're still blowing out your PC with canned air, you're doing it wrong — and spending too much over time. A cordless electric air duster is currently available for $19, and it's the kind of purchase that makes you wonder why you ever tolerated the alternatives.

## Why Canned Air Is a Bad Deal

A single can of compressed air costs between $5 and $10 and lasts maybe three or four good cleanings before it's empty. Over a year of regular PC maintenance, you'd easily spend $40 or more on a consumable product that generates waste and loses pressure as it empties. The math doesn't lie.

## What You Get for $19

This cordless duster is rechargeable, reusable, and delivers consistent airflow without the diminishing returns of a can. It's lighter than you'd expect, quiet enough for indoor use, and powerful enough to clear dust from heatsinks, fans, and hard-to-reach crevices. For PC builders and anyone who keeps a desktop within arm's reach, it's the kind of practical upgrade that should've been standard years ago.
