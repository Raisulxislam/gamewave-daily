Elden Ring's Tarnished Edition has arrived, and with it a fresher wardrobe for the Lands Between — new armor sets and weapons tucked away for veterans to hunt down. The good news is that the hunt is very much on.

## Where the New Gear Is Hiding

Much of the new loot isn't going to be handed over at a merchant's stall. Expect to earn it the hard way, the Elden Ring way — from guarded chests, ambushing bosses, and tucked-away corners of the map that punish anyone who rushes straight through. Keeping an eye on your map for unmarked paths and hidden graces is half the battle.

## Armor and Weapons Worth Your Time

While the specific locations stay deliberately cryptic, the general wisdom holds: explore before you fight. New weapons often live behind legacy dungeons and optional bosses, while the flashiest armor tends to demand you commit to the game's toughest encounters. If you're willing to wander, the Tarnished Edition rewards exactly the kind of thoroughness the base game always celebrated.
