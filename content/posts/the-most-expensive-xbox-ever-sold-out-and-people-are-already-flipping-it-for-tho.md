Microsoft's 25th Anniversary Xbox Series X — the most expensive Xbox console ever made — sold out within a minute of preorders going live. The limited-edition hardware, priced well above the standard Series X, was gone before most buyers could even complete checkout.

## Scalpers Strike Before the Dust Settles

Predictably, the reseller market moved fast. Within hours of the sellout, listings appeared on secondary markets asking two to three times the original retail price. The pattern is painfully familiar: limited hardware drops create instant secondary-market premiums, and the 25th Anniversary Xbox — with its scarcity and collector appeal — was an obvious target.

Microsoft hasn't commented on the sellout speed or potential restock plans. For collectors who actually wanted to own the thing rather than flip it, the experience underscores a frustration that's plagued limited-edition hardware drops for years: the people most excited about a product are often the last ones to get their hands on it.
