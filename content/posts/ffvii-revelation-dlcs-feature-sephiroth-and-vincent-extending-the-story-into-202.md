## Sephiroth and Vincent headline FFVII Revelation's post-launch plans

Just hours after Final Fantasy VII Revelation's release date was confirmed as April 8 during the PlayStation State of Play, two story DLC expansions appeared on Steam — and they signal a much longer narrative road than expected. The first expansion centers on Sephiroth, while the second focuses on Vincent Valentine, two characters whose intertwined histories promise to push the Revelation storyline well into 2028.

## Steam leaked the roadmap before Square Enix could

The listings on Steam suggest Square Enix was planning a staggered rollout of post-launch content, though the timing of the appearance hints that either a premature upload or an eager storefront pushed the details out ahead of schedule. Either way, fans now know the story doesn't end with the base game — and the scope of what's coming could reshape expectations for the full Revelation experience.
