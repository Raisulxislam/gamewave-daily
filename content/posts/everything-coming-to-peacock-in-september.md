September is that sweet spot on the streaming calendar — summer's heat finally breaks and the big fall premieres start rolling in. Peacock is leaning hard into both moods with a jam-packed Peacock September lineup that blends original debuts, returning favorites, and a horror backlog that gets spookier as the month rolls on.

## Originals worth circling

The Office spin-off The Paper returns for Season 2 on Sept. 9, dropping all episodes at once for a proper binge. Reality fans get The Traitors: New Blood with Alan Cumming back commanding the Scottish highlands on Sept. 18, and the new Peter Krause-led thriller Line of Fire arrives Sept. 22.

## The movie dump

Sept. 1 is a serious drop: the entire Twilight saga, all three Tremors films, Dracula, Get Out, The Goonies, and a wall of horror classics land together. NBC stalwarts like Saturday Night Live Season 52 and The Voice Season 30 round out a month that has a little of everything.
