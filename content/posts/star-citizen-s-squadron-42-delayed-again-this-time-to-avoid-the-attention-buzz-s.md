Squadron 42, the star-packed singleplayer space FPS at the heart of the Star Citizen saga, has slipped past yet another release window. The news lands quietly but no less painfully for fans who have waited since its original 2015 target — more than a decade of hoping, and still no concrete date on the calendar.

## A Decade in the Making

The game was first planned to ship back in 2015, and every intervening year has tested the patience of even the most devoted backers. Each delay has come with its own explanation, but this one carries a distinct flavor: Chris Roberts, the project's longtime figurehead, has pointed at none other than Grand Theft Auto 6, which he fears could become a colossal 'attention buzz saw' that would swallow any competing launch whole.

## Fair or Convenient?

It's a rare admission that even Star Citizen — famous for its own towering hype machine — doesn't want to go head-to-head with Rockstar's juggernaut. The reasoning is commercially sound: few games survive a direct collision with GTA 6's marketing gravity. But after so many missed windows, the lingering question is whether the delay is about timing, or simply another chapter in a story that never quite seems to reach its ending.

Either way, backers are left to do what they've always done — wait, watch the funding ticker climb, and hope that the next delay, whenever it comes, is the last.
