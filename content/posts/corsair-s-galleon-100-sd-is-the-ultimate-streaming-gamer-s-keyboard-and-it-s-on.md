## Built for Streamers Who Game

Corsair is betting that streamers want a single keyboard that does everything — and the **GALLEON 100 SD** is its answer. Launching today with an introductory sale price, the board packs dedicated streaming controls, Corsair's latest switch technology, and the kind of macro density that competitive players expect.

The GALLEON 100 SD sits at the intersection of broadcast convenience and gaming performance. Dedicated stream deck–style keys mean fewer alt-tabs mid-broadcast, while the underlying hardware targets the low-latency response that fast-paced titles demand.

It's not Corsair's first swing at the streamer market, but it's the most integrated one yet — and the launch-day pricing makes it worth a look for anyone currently juggling a separate keyboard and stream controller.
