CD Projekt has confirmed that The Witcher Remake, being developed by Fool's Theory, has been placed on the back burner. The studio is prioritizing work on The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt's Songs of the Past expansion while waiting for key technological innovations to come out of The Witcher 4 development pipeline.

## Why the Wait Makes Strategic Sense

The decision is a pragmatic one. Building a next-generation remake without first understanding the full capabilities of the engine powering The Witcher 4 would risk redundant work. By letting the larger project's technology mature, Fool's Theory can eventually build the remake with tools and systems already battle-tested in a flagship title.

## Fans Will Have to Be Patient

For a community that has been eagerly awaiting the return of the original game in modern form, the news is a mixed bag. On one hand, a remake built on cutting-edge tech will be worth the wait. On the other, the original Witcher game grows more inaccessible by the year, and every delay extends that gap.
