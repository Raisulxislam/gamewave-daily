## A First-Person Trip Through Hell — Literally

A brand-new first-person shooter just announced its intent to drop players straight into the nightmarish vistas of Hieronymus Bosch, the 15th-century Netherlandish painter whose hallucinatory canvases have fascinated and horrified audiences for over five hundred years. The game promises to let you fight your way through reimagined versions of his most famous works, and if the early materials are anything to go by, the studio is leaning hard into the grotesque.

## Will the Butt Song From Hell Make the Cut?

For the uninitiated, Bosch's *The Garden of Earthly Delights* contains one of art history's most bizarre recurring images: a pair of ears jammed between blades, with a knife splitting them open, accompanied by what internet culture has lovingly dubbed "the 500-year-old butt song from hell" — a piece of musical notation painted onto the figure's backside. Whether the game will incorporate this gloriously weird detail remains to be seen, but the devs clearly know their audience. Bosch's work has always rewarded close, obsessive inspection, and this FPS sounds like it wants to do the same — just with more firepower.
