September's streaming slate is unusually stacked for a transitional month. Between a franchise spinoff, a legacy sequel, and a prestige sci-fi finale, there's something arriving almost every week that's worth clearing your schedule for.

## South Park Returns, and Hillman College Gets a New Chapter

South Park kicks off its 29th season on September 16 on Comedy Central, with episodes streaming next-day on Paramount+. The biweekly release cadence stretches the six-episode run through November 25, giving Trey Parker and Matt Stone their usual tight turnaround to keep the satire current. Meanwhile, Netflix drops A Different World on September 24 — a legacy sequel to the beloved HBCU-set sitcom, following Whitley and Dwayne's daughter as she carves her own identity at Hillman. The single-camera revival brings back Jasmine Guy, Kadeem Hardison, and Debbie Allen alongside new faces, which is about as strong a nostalgia play as any streamer could ask for.

## The Reacher Universe Expands, and Strange New Worlds Says Goodbye

Prime Video drops Reacher spinoff Neagley on September 16 — the same day Reacher Season 4 wraps — giving Maria Sten's fan-favorite character her own investigation into a friend's suspicious death. All eight episodes land at once, making it a binge-ready companion piece. Over on Paramount+, Strange New Worlds Season 4 finale "Tomorrow's Enterprise" lands later in the month, setting up the show's abbreviated fifth and final season. It's a bittersweet moment for Star Trek's most warmly received modern series, and the finale's title alone is enough to set the internet theorizing.
