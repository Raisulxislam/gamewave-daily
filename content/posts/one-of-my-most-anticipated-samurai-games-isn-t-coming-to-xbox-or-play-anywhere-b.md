## A dark samurai CRPG with a date at last

One of my most anticipated samurai games has finally told us when it's coming — even if the news comes with some platform heartbreak. Shadow of the Road, the turn-based CRPG published by Owlcat Games and set in a dark, steampunk-fantasy reimagining of feudal Japan, will launch on November 12, 2026, for PC.

## Why the PC-only news stings

The catch: it won't be coming to Xbox, and it's skipping Play Anywhere compatibility. For anyone who'd been hoping to take this tale of ronin and steam-powered horror across other screens, that's a real letdown — and it's why this long-awaited date arrives with a little bitterness mixed in.

Still, a locked release date is a locked release date. Fans of grim tactical RPGs now have a firm day on the calendar to mark, even if it's on PC only.
