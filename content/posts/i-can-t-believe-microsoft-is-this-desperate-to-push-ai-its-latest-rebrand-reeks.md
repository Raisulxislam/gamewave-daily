## The AI at Work Roadmap is a naming exercise

Microsoft has quietly renamed its long-running **Microsoft 365 Roadmap** to the **"AI at Work Roadmap,"** and honestly? It reads as marketing spin on empty calories. The catch is that most of what the tracker actually catalogues has nothing to do with AI at all — it's the same rollout and feature-update rundown it's always been, just wearing a shinier, trendier label.

## Desperation disguised as vision

There's a defensible case that AI now touches a growing slice of the product line. But painting an entire update log with an AI brush is the kind of decision that says more about a company's need to look forward-looking than it does about genuine product strategy. When a roadmap's name spends more time chasing the buzzword than describing what's on it, users have every right to roll their eyes.

The underlying issue isn't that Microsoft likes AI — plenty of people do. It's that this feels like a rebrand built around perception rather than substance, and the corpo-speak does no favours to the developers and admins who actually live in that roadmap.
