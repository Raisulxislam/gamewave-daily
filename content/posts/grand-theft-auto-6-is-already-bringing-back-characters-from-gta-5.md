## Familiar faces are returning to the series

Rockstar is already drafting its reunion tour. New reports indicate **GTA 6** will feature at least one character pulled straight from **GTA Online** — a quiet but telling nod to longtime fans who've spent the better part of a decade in Los Santos.

It's a smart move on Rockstar's part. Rather than starting entirely from scratch in the new state of Leonida, the developer is apparently banking on continuity, giving players a thread to follow that links the sprawling multiplayer world back to the single-player future.

## A smarter hook for returning players

Don't expect a full roster of favourites just yet — the early word points to a single confirmed cameo-style appearance. But if Rockstar is threading even one Online veteran into the main campaign, it sets an intriguing precedent for how the two corners of the franchise could keep leaning on each other. The big unanswerable question remains just how deep those connections run by the time we finally get to play.
