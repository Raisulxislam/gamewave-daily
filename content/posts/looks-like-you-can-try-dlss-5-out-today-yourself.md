## The next-gen upscaler is here

It looks like players can get their hands on DLSS 5 themselves starting today. If the rollout lands as expected, the latest version of Nvidia's upscaling technology will be available to those willing to fire it up in supported titles.

## A smarter name for a bigger story

The intriguing part isn't just the tech — it's the marketing. Nvidia appears to be doing the smart thing by rebranding the whole DLSS 5 pitch as "DLSS Neural Rendering." That's more than a cosmetic change. It reframes a single upscaling feature as a broader, AI-driven rendering platform, signaling that the real story here isn't one trick, but a full stack of neural graphics tools.

For anyone who loves to chase the bleeding edge, today could be the day to plug in, load up a compatible game, and see what DLSS Neural Rendering actually looks like in motion.
