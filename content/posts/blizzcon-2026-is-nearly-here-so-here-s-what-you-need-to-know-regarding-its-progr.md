BlizzCon 2026 is officially on the horizon. Blizzard Entertainment has published a comprehensive guide covering everything from show floor logistics to streaming options, giving fans and attendees a clear picture of what to expect when the convention opens its doors on September 12.

## The Programming Schedule

This year's lineup promises a heavy dose of World of Warcraft, Overwatch 2, and Diablo IV announcements alongside what Blizzard describes as "a few surprises" — language that reliably sends the internet into speculation overdrive. Panels, esports showcases, and developer Q&As are spread across both days of the event.

## Floor Plan and Streaming

For those heading to the venue in person, Blizzard has released a detailed floor map with dedicated zones for hands-on demos, meet-and-greets, and the main stage. Remote fans won't miss out: streams will be available on YouTube, Twitch, and Blizzard's own website in multiple languages. The company confirmed that all major panels will feature live English commentary with subtitled feeds available shortly after.

With BlizzCon historically serving as a launchpad for major announcements, this edition could be one of the most consequential in recent memory. The full guide is live on Blizzard's official site now.
