Prime Video has pulled the plug on *Spider-Noir* after just one season. The series, which starred Nicolas Cage as an aging private eye in a 1930s-set Spider-Man universe, debuted to strong critical reception earlier this year but apparently didn't convert enough viewers to justify a second chapter.

## Why It Mattered

*Spider-Noir* was one of the more ambitious swings in Prime Video's Marvel-adjacent slate. It wasn't a retread of the MCU playbook — it was a genuinely different thing, a period piece that used the superhero framework to tell a hard-boiled detective story. Cage's performance earned widespread praise, and the show proved that superhero fatigue doesn't apply when you actually do something fresh with the genre. That's what makes this cancellation sting: the creative ambition was clearly there.

The show was part of Sony's broader push to expand its Spider-Man IP beyond Tom Holland's films. With *Spider-Noir* gone, it raises questions about how much appetite Sony and Amazon actually have for experimental corners of that universe. **One thing is clear: a great cast and good reviews aren't always enough to survive the streaming math.**
