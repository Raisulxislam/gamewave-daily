## GTA 6 fuel leak inspires a GTA 5 mod

A modder has recreated a car fuelling system reportedly modeled on GTA 6 leaked mechanics, and has ported it into Grand Theft Auto 5. The mod introduces realistic fuel consumption, empty-tank consequences, and the kind of driving tension that GTA 6 leaks have hinted at — giving curious players a hands-on preview of what the next entry might demand behind the wheel.

## Community reacts to the crossover experiment

The mod has sparked conversation across GTA communities about whether a fuel system would enhance or frustrate the series' driving experience. Some players welcome the added realism; others argue it would clash with GTA's arcade-paced identity. Either way, it's a fascinating proof of concept — and a reminder that the modding community moves fast, even faster than Rockstar sometimes.
