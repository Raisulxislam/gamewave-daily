Samsung's **57-inch Odyssey Neo G9** — one of the most absurdly large gaming monitors on the market — has dropped to what appears to be its all-time lowest price. If you've been waiting for a sign to pull the trigger on a dual-4K display that essentially replaces a dual-monitor setup in a single panel, this might be it.

## Why This Monitor Stands Out

With a 57-inch curved panel running at a staggering 7680 x 2160 resolution, this display delivers two full 4K monitors' worth of screen space without the bezel gap. It supports a 240Hz refresh rate, making it competitive with far smaller high-end panels on pure speed. For flight-sim enthusiasts, sim racers, and anyone who's ever wished their single monitor was just a *little* bit wider, it's a dream spec sheet.

## The Price Reality Check

At its original asking price, the Odyssey Neo G9 was firmly in luxury territory. The current discount brings it into more accessible — if still not cheap — range. For a display of this size and specification, the value proposition is suddenly a lot more compelling than it was at launch.
