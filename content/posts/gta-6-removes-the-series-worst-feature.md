## Old sprint mechanic gets the boot

If you've ever mashed a face button just to sprint across a city block in Grand Theft Auto, you know the pain. Rockstar is reportedly retiring the franchise's long-criticized sprint system in GTA 6, replacing face-button mashing with a standard left-stick click — the same L3 input most modern games use for running.

## Quality-of-life upgrades signal a modernized GTA 6

It's one of those changes that sounds minor on paper but fundamentally alters how the game feels moment to moment. The old system was a relic from an era when controller conventions hadn't settled; GTA 6 acknowledges that era is over. Combined with other reported quality-of-life improvements, Rockstar seems intent on making sure the controls don't distract from the experience — they enhance it.
